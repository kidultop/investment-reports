Anthropic 内部研究版 Claude 在黎曼猜想相关问题上取得进展
An internal research build of Claude advanced a result tied to the Riemann Hypothesis
Anthropic 一名员工提示一个尚未公开发布的研究版 Claude "认真试一下"黎曼猜想。模型未证出猜想本身，但在相关方向上将"满足黎曼猜想的零点比例"的已知下界从 41.6% 提升到 67.2%。工作在两个会话中完成，共消耗约 3100 万输出 token，协调约 60 个子智能体、执行 2400 条 shell 命令、编写数百个 Python 脚本，并从 arXiv 下载 54 篇论文确认结论此前未发表。结果已通过 Lean 形式化验证，并由 Anthropic 两位数学家审阅。Anthropic 强调该方法预计不会直接导向猜想的最终证明。
An Anthropic employee prompted an unreleased research build of Claude to "seriously try" the Riemann Hypothesis. The model did not prove the conjecture, but in a side direction it raised the known lower bound on the proportion of zeros satisfying the hypothesis from 41.6% to 67.2%. The work spanned two sessions, ~31M output tokens, ~60 sub-agents, 2,400 shell commands and hundreds of Python scripts, with 54 arXiv papers downloaded to confirm novelty. A Lean formalization passed standard verification; two Anthropic mathematicians reviewed it. Anthropic stresses the method is not expected to lead directly to a final proof.
为何重要 Why it matters：
这是前沿模型首次在数学最难题之一的相关方向上，由模型自主驱动、经形式化验证地推进了已知下界，凸显长程自主推理与子智能体编排的成熟度；同时也暴露模型最初因"习得难度"而自我怀疑，需人类鼓励才推进。
It is the first time a frontier model autonomously advanced a known bound on one of mathematics' hardest problems, with formal verification — a marker of mature long-horizon reasoning and sub-agent orchestration, while also revealing models' learned self-doubt that required human encouragement to overcome.

arXiv: Distributed Safety Alignment Against White-Box Attacks

An Aug 4 arXiv paper introduces "No Single Neuron of Failure," a distributed safety-alignment method that spreads safety constraints across components rather than a single neuron, improving robustness against white-box attacks (where the adversary knows model weights). It outperforms single-point alignment baselines under such attacks.
为何重要 Why it matters：
