SpaceX's $101 Billion Unlock Heaps Pressure on Battered Shares
SpaceX 1010亿美元限售股解禁，给 battered 股价带来更大压力
Investors hanging onto SpaceX shares after a plunge below their IPO price are bracing for the next potential hit, when $101 billion worth of stock becomes available for trading on Thursday. FT: Fed Chair Warsh Prepared to Hike in September if Inflation Data Runs Hot
金融时报：美联储主席沃什准备在9月加息，若通胀数据偏热
According to the Financial Times, Federal Reserve Chair Kevin Warsh is prepared to raise interest rates at the September meeting if inflation data in the coming weeks comes in hotter than expected. reuters.com⭐⭐⭐⭐
Fed's Musalem: Monetary Policy Must Maintain Substantial Restriction on Inflation
美联储穆萨莱姆：货币政策须对通胀维持实质性抑制
St. Louis Fed President Alberto Musalem said it is crucial that monetary policy impose a significant restriction on underlying inflation, rather than tolerate slightly higher inflation today in pursuit of productivity growth tomorrow. "Inflation is well above the FOMC's 2% target, and the balance of risks points to inflation remaining above target a year or more from now," Musalem said. He warned that a central bank seen as tolerant of above-target inflation risks undermining its credibility anchor.
圣路易斯联储主席阿尔贝托·穆萨莱姆表示，关键是货币政策要对核心通胀施加重大限制，而不是为了明天追求生产率增长就忍受今天稍高的通胀。"通胀远高于FOMC 2%的目标，风险平衡指向通胀将在一年或更长时间内保持高于目标，"穆萨莱姆说。他警告称，被视为容忍高于目标通胀的央行可能危及其公信力锚定。

Five Federal Reserve Regional Bank Presidents Support Rate Hike After July Meeting
五名美联储地区联储主席在7月会议后支持加息
As of August 5, five Federal Reserve regional bank presidents have supported a rate hike following the July meeting, citing a mildly accommodative policy stance and persistent inflation risks. Harker of Cleveland, Logan of Dallas, Kashkari of Minneapolis, Schmid of Kansas City, and Musalem of St. Louis all back tighter rates. They point to core inflation near 2.5%, robust demand, and concerns about market confidence in the Fed.
截至8月5日，五名美联储地区联储主席在7月会议后公开支持加息，理由是政策立场仍然偏宽松、通胀风险持续存在。克利夫兰的哈克、达拉斯的洛根、明尼阿波利斯卡什卡里、堪萨斯城的施密德和圣路易斯的穆萨莱姆均支持收紧利率。他们指出核心通胀接近2.5%、需求强劲，并担忧市场对美联储的信心。

Iran Drafts Bill to Bar US, Israel Ships from Strait of Hormuz
伊朗起草法案禁止美国、以色列船只通过霍尔木兹海峡
Iran's parliament is reviewing a draft bill that would ban US, Israeli and other 'hostile' vessels from passing through the Strait of Hormuz. The draft, titled 'Strategic Actions to Ensure Security and Sustainable Development of the Strait of Hormuz and the Persian Gulf,' would also prohibit goods related to Israel, both military and civilian, from passing through the region. Violators could face fines of up to 20% of cargo value.
伊朗议会正在审议一项草案法案，拟禁止美国、以色列及其他"敌对"船只通过霍尔木兹海峡。该草案名为《确保霍尔木兹海峡和波斯湾安全与可持续发展的战略行动》，还将禁止与以色列相关的军用和民用货物通过该地区。违规者可能面临最高相当于货物价值20%的罚款。
