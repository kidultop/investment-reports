Goldman Says Japan's AI Stock Trade 'Not Broken' After Rout
高盛：日本AI股票交易在暴跌后并未“崩盘”
Goldman Sachs Japan Co. says a sharp selloff in Japan's artificial intelligence-related stocks has created a buying opportunity, arguing that strong earnings could revive investor appetite for semiconductor shares. SK Hynix Q2 profit jumps 557% on AI chip demand, misses forecasts
SK海力士Q2营业利润暴增557%创纪录，但仍低于预期
South Korean chipmaker SK Hynix reported a 557% increase in quarterly operating profit to a record high on Wednesday, driven by robust demand for advanced memory chips as big technology firms ramped up spending on AI data centres. The Nvidia supplier reported an operating profit of 60.5 trillion won ($41.62 billion) for the April-June period, compared with 9.2 trillion won a year earlier. That compared with a 64 trillion won forecast by LSEG SmartEstimate. SK Hynix missed analysts' forecasts because its higher exposure to high-end memory chips used in AI data centres meant it benefited less from a stronger price rally in conventional memory chips.
韩国芯片制造商SK海力士周三公布，受AI数据中心支出强劲推动，二季度营业利润同比暴增557%至创纪录的60.5万亿韩元（约合416亿美元），但仍低于LSEG SmartEstimate预测的64万亿韩元。公司解释称，由于其产品组合更偏重于AI数据中心用高端存储芯片，因此在传统存储芯片价格反弹中受益较少。

S&P 500 ends higher as investors await tech earnings
科技股财报前标普500收高，芯片股下挫
The S&P 500 ended higher on Tuesday as gains in Boeing and Coca-Cola helped offset tumbling chip stocks ahead of quarterly reports from Apple and other tech companies this week. Global markets have been volatile this month as investors worry that Alphabet, Microsoft, Amazon and other technology heavyweights may be overspending on AI data centers. Microsoft rose 1.1% ahead of its report on Wednesday, while Amazon dipped 0.2% ahead of its results on Thursday. Apple climbed almost 1% to $340.08, briefly lifting its stock market value to $5 trillion for the first time. The PHLX semiconductor index lost 4.5% and has fallen about 25% from its record-high close on June 22. Coca-Cola rallied 5% after raising its annual revenue and profit forecasts. Traders see a 71% probability that the Fed will leave rates unchanged and a 29% chance of a 25-basis-point rate hike.
周二标普500指数收高，波音和可口可乐上涨抵消了芯片股大跌；苹果、微软等大型科技公司本周将陆续发布财报。本月全球市场波动加剧，投资者担心Alphabet、微软、亚马逊等科技巨头在AI数据中心上的支出过度。微软周三财报前上涨1.1%，苹果股价接近1%涨至340.08美元，盘中市值一度突破5万亿美元。费城半导体指数下跌4.5%，较6月22日高点回落约25%。可口可乐因上调全年营收和利润预期大涨5%。交易员预计美联储维持利率不变的概率为71%，加息25个基点的概率为29%。
