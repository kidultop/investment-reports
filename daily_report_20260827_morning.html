Oil little changed after Iran says deal reached with Oman to share Hormuz revenue
伊朗称与阿曼达成霍尔木兹收入分享协议,油价本周累跌超5%
Iran's Revolutionary Guard said Tehran and Oman reached a deal to share revenue generated from the Strait of Hormuz, suggesting some type of transit fee is planned. The Guard said the U.S. must accept the agreement for Hormuz to reopen. Brent crude fell 74 cents to close at $87.84 a barrel while WTI lost 13 cents to settle at $82.23. Oil fell more than 3% earlier in the session as the U.S. relies on economic pressure against Iran rather than military strikes. Prices are down more than 5% for the week. RBC's Helima Croft said it is unclear whether the U.S. would sign off on Iran jointly managing Hormuz, and Gulf nations attacked by Iran during the war are not going to pay Tehran to ship oil through the strait. Trump said 10 million barrels exited the strait on Tuesday.
伊朗革命卫队发言人向官方通讯社Tasnim表示,德黑兰与阿曼已就霍尔木兹海峡达成收入分享协议(暗示将对过境船只收费),并称美国必须接受该协议霍尔木兹才能重新开放。油价方面:Brent收跌74美分至87.84美元/桶(-0.84%),WTI跌13美分至82.23美元(-0.16%);盘中一度跌超3%(美国对伊朗转向经济施压而非军事打击),本周累计跌超5%。RBC资本市场的Helima Croft分析:美国是否会同意伊朗联合管理霍尔木兹海峡尚不清楚,且在战争期间遭伊朗攻击的海湾国家不会向德黑兰付费通行。特朗普称周二有1000万桶石油流出海峡。船只按协议从伊朗水域进入波斯湾,经阿曼与伊朗领海联合走廊驶出。

特朗普向国会提交沙特民用核能协议,前提是沙特加入亚伯拉罕协议
President Donald Trump submitted a proposed agreement with Saudi Arabia to develop civil nuclear energy to Congress for approval. Salesforce CEO Benioff回击SaaS末日论,公司在AI转型中备战财报

Nvidia Q2 revenue more than doubles to $96.2 billion, Q3 guidance tops $1 trillion run rate
英伟达Q2营收翻倍至962亿美元超预期,Q3指引首破千亿美元,盘后涨4.7%
Nvidia reported fiscal Q2 revenue of $96.22 billion, up 106% year over year and beating the $92.17 billion consensus. Adjusted EPS came in at $2.22 versus $2.10 expected. Data center revenue surged 117% to $89 billion, with hyperscaler revenue at $48.7 billion (+102%). The company guided Q3 revenue to $108 billion plus or minus 2%, above the $105.2 billion consensus, marking the first quarter above $1 trillion annualized run rate. CFO Colette Kress said Nvidia expects fiscal 2028 revenue to grow about 70%, far above street expectations of 44%, citing supply constraints as the main limiter. Shares initially fell 4% after hours on soft gross margin guidance (74% vs 75%) but reversed to gain 4.7% at $219.53. Amazon committed to an additional 2 million GPUs. Nvidia disclosed $33.5 billion in senior notes and a $25 billion commercial paper program, with $15 billion due within 1-5 years, flagging indebtedness as a risk factor.
英伟达2027财年第二季度营收962.2亿美元,同比增长106%,超出市场预期的921.7亿;调整后每股收益2.22美元(预期2.10)。数据中心营收890亿美元(+117%),其中超大规模客户贡献487亿(+102%)、AI云/企业客户403亿(+138%)。Q3营收指引1080亿美元±2%(超共识1052亿),意味着单季营收首次突破千亿美元。CFO克蕾丝表示2028财年营收预计增长约70%,远超分析师预期的44%,供应瓶颈仍是主要制约。盘后股价先跌4%(毛利率指引74%略低于75%)后反转大涨4.7%至219.53美元。亚马逊追加采购200万块GPU;黄仁勋称AI已到拐点、算力即收入;公司联合Apollo/贝莱德/黑石/高盛/KKR计划调动超5000亿美元第三方资本建设AI基础设施。风险披露:335亿优先票据+250亿商业票据计划,150亿债务1-5年内到期。

Debasement trade to take bitcoin to $300,000 in three years, Bernstein says
Bernstein:贬值交易将推动比特币三年内达到30万美元
Analysts at Bernstein said the so-called debasement trade — where investors buy bitcoin and gold as a hedge against a weaker U.S. dollar and mounting Treasury debt — will take bitcoin to $300,000 within three years. The thesis has found fresh momentum following Treasury Secretary Scott Bessent's intervention in the bond market, as investors look to hedge against dollar weakness and U.S. fiscal deterioration. Bitcoin traded above $81,000 this week, its strongest level in months, with IBIT ETF seeing inflows of $1.92 billion last week, the largest since October.
Bernstein分析师表示,所谓「贬值交易」——投资者买入比特币和黄金对冲美元走弱与美国国债债务膨胀——将在三年内推动比特币升至30万美元。该论题在财政部长贝森特干预债市后获得新动力,投资者寻求对冲美元疲软和美国财政恶化风险。比特币本周交投于81,000美元上方,为数月来最强水平;IBIT ETF上周净流入19.2亿美元,创去年10月以来最大单周流入。黄金亦同步走强至4,700美元附近的历史高位区域,「黄金+比特币」组合成为对冲主权货币贬值的经典配置。

Fed's preferred inflation gauge shows core prices rose 3.3% annually in July
美联储青睐的PCE指标:7月核心PCE同比涨3.3%,距2%目标仍然遥远
The personal consumption expenditures price index rose 0.2% for the month, putting annual inflation at 3.7%, both 0.1 percentage point above the Dow Jones consensus. Core PCE posted gains of 0.2% monthly and 3.3% annually, in line with forecasts. Personal income rose 0.4% and spending increased 0.2%, both stronger than expected. Goods prices fell 0.1% on a 2.7% drop in gasoline, while services rose 0.3% led by financial services (+1.2%). Markets are pricing in only about a 1-in-3 probability of a hike at the September 15-16 FOMC, with the best chance for a rate hike coming in December. The 10- and 30-year Treasury yields recently hit their highest levels since 2007.
美联储首选通胀指标显示:7月PCE环比+0.2%、同比+3.7%,均高于道琼斯共识0.1个百分点;核心PCE环比+0.2%、同比+3.3%符合预期,但仍远高于2%目标。个人收入+0.4%、支出+0.2%,均强于预期。商品价格环比-0.1%(汽油-2.7%),服务+0.3%(金融保险+1.2%)。市场定价9月15-16日FOMC加息概率仅约三分之一,12月加息概率最高。10年期和30年期美债收益率近期创2007年以来新高。通胀数据公布后美股期货小幅回落、美债收益率走高。这是沃什周五杰克逊霍尔讲话前最后一份重磅通胀数据。

Dollar and bond markets on edge ahead of Jackson Hole as Bessent intervention piles pressure on Warsh
杰克逊霍尔前夕美元与债市如坐针毡:贝森特干预债市给沃什施压,BofA警告30Y或冲5.5%
Bank of America labeled the Jackson Hole symposium a key risk event, warning that the dollar is vulnerable to an extended sell-off if Fed Chair Kevin Warsh disappoints markets. BofA strategists said they expect Warsh to change his communication to help contain the bond market, fearing a rapid long-end bond yield rise to 5.5% or higher if he does not, and warned that a speech focused solely on structural themes like productivity or demographics could be interpreted as dovish. Morgan Stanley economists said Warsh is sincere in his desire to communicate less and will not clarify his near-term outlook. Stifel expects a dovish message steepening the yield curve and weakening the dollar. Invesco's Benjamin Jones said a dovish speech would reinforce dollar weakness, gold strength and curve steepening, while the path of least resistance is for higher U.S. yields given resilient nominal growth, persistent inflation risk, and competition for capital from the AI investment boom. The 30-year Treasury was at 5.173% and the 10-year around 4.64%.
美银(BofA)将杰克逊霍尔年会列为「关键风险事件」,警告若美联储主席沃什令市场失望,美元面临延续性抛售风险。BofA策略师称期待沃什改变沟通方式以帮助控制债市——若没有,担忧长端美债收益率快速升至5.5%以上;若演讲仅聚焦生产率、人口等结构性大主题,市场可能解读为鸽派。摩根士丹利认为沃什真心想减少沟通,不会给出近期经济和政策展望。Stifel预期鸽派信息将使收益率曲线陡峭化、美元走弱。景顺Benjamin Jones指出:鸽派演讲将强化美元弱、黄金强、曲线陡化逻辑,且在美国名义增长韧性、通胀持续风险、AI投资热潮争夺资本背景下,美债收益率的阻力最小路径是上行。当前30年期美债收益率5.173%、10年期约4.64%,逼近关键位。
