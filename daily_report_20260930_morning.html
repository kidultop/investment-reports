US consumer confidence near 12-1/2-year low amid labor market fears
美国消费者信心降至近12年半低点，劳动力市场担忧加剧
US consumer confidence plunged to a near 12-1/2-year low in September, with households expecting a deterioration in business and labor market conditions over the next six months. The Conference Board index plummeted 6.7 points to 81.9, the weakest since April 2014 and below the 89.2 forecast. A separate Labor Department report showed job openings dropped by 256,000 to 7.079 million in August. The Federal Reserve raised interest rates earlier this month and traders now see around a 70% chance of another hike in October.
9月美国消费者信心骤降至近12年半低点，家庭预计未来六个月商业和劳动力市场状况将恶化。世界大型企业研究会指数暴跌6.7点至81.9，为2014年4月以来最低，远低于预期的89.2。劳工部另一报告显示，8月职位空缺减少25.6万至707.9万个。美联储本月已加息，交易员目前预计10月再次加息的概率约为70%。

Oil prices fall on signs Middle East crude flows recovering
中东原油流量出现复苏迹象，油价下跌
Oil prices declined on Tuesday as investors focused on signs of recovering crude exports from the Middle East. Brent crude futures settled lower and US West Texas Intermediate crude also fell. Saudi Arabia resumed oil tanker loadings from its Red Sea port of Yanbu after restarting operations on the East-West Pipeline, improving the outlook for oil exports from the region. Crude exports from key Middle East producers rebounded in September to the highest level since the conflict started, according to Kpler data.
周二油价下跌，因投资者关注中东原油出口复苏迹象。布伦特原油期货收跌，美国西德克萨斯中质原油也走低。沙特在东西管道恢复运营后，重启了红海延布港的石油装船，改善了该地区石油出口前景。Kpler数据显示，9月中东主要产油国原油出口反弹至冲突爆发以来最高水平。

Bond yields crank higher and pull US stocks further from their record
美债收益率攀升，美股进一步远离纪录高位
Yields in the US bond market touched their highest levels in roughly two decades, knocking US stocks further from their record high. The S&P 500 fell 0.8% and the Nasdaq composite sank 0.9%. The yield on the 10-year Treasury briefly topped 5.27% before pulling back to 5.23%, while the 30-year Treasury yield rose to 5.55%. Gold miners were weak after the price of gold sank 3.5%, and Nvidia climbed 1.7% after announcing an expanded share buyback plan.
美国债市收益率触及约20年来最高水平，美股进一步远离纪录高位。标普500指数下跌0.8%，纳斯达克综合指数下跌0.9%。10年期美债收益率一度突破5.27%后回落至5.23%，30年期美债收益率升至5.55%。金价下跌3.5%拖累黄金矿商股，英伟达在宣布扩大股票回购计划后上涨1.7%。

RBA Hikes Key Rate to 15-Year High
澳洲联储将关键利率上调至15年高点
The Reserve Bank of Australia raised its cash rate target by 25 basis points to 4.60% in a unanimous September 2026 decision, in line with expectations. Policymakers noted that higher global energy prices, stronger-than-expected inflation and persistent domestic capacity constraints are adding to price pressures. AI-related demand is also driving increases in global technology goods prices, while firms report rising costs and plans to pass costs on. The Board said further tightening may be needed to return inflation sustainably to target.
澳洲联储在2026年9月会议上全票通过加息25个基点，将现金利率目标上调至4.60%，符合市场预期。决策者指出，全球能源价格上涨、通胀强于预期以及国内产能约束持续，正在加剧物价压力。与人工智能相关的需求也推升全球科技商品价格，企业报告成本上升并计划转嫁成本。委员会表示，可能需进一步收紧政策以令通胀可持续回归目标。
