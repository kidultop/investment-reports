Japan spent record 27.1 trillion yen on currency support in 2026, reserves slide to $1.2 trillion
日本年内干预汇市支出创纪录27.1万亿日元，外储降至1.2万亿美元
Japan has spent 27.1 trillion yen supporting the currency so far in 2026, the largest annual intervention total on record and above the previous 20.4 trillion yen peak set in 2003. August alone saw a monthly record 15.4 trillion yen deployed through Aug. 26, part of a joint operation with the United States, cutting foreign reserves by about $80 billion to $1.207 trillion. Strategists warn the unprecedented scale of carry-trade exposure is forcing caution: outstanding cross-border yen lending has grown from 216 trillion yen in December 2021 to 360 trillion yen as of March, making this the largest accumulation of carry positions in three decades and raising the risk of a disorderly unwind if the BOJ tightens faster than expected.
日本2026年迄今已投入27.1万亿日元支撑汇率，为有记录以来最大年度干预规模，超过2003年创下的20.4万亿日元前高。仅8月一个月（截至8月26日）就投入创纪录的15.4万亿日元，其中部分为与美国的联合行动，令外汇储备减少约800亿美元至1.207万亿美元。策略师警告，前所未有的套息交易敞口规模正迫使官方保持谨慎：未偿付跨境日元借贷已由2021年12月的216万亿日元增至今年3月的360万亿日元，为三十年来最大的套息头寸积累，若日央行收紧快于预期，将显著提高无序平仓的风险。

Yen jumps to strongest since February, breaking key 155 level as BOJ bets build
日元升至2月来最强，突破155关键关口，加息押注升温
The yen surged as much as 1.4% to 154.06 per dollar in Monday's London session, its strongest level since February and above the peak reached after a previous round of coordinated Japan-U.S. intervention. Breaking 155 was technically significant because that level had acted as a floor after past interventions, triggering a cascade of stop-loss orders and forcing option dealers to sell dollars. The yen is up roughly 3.3% in five sessions. BOJ board member Hajime Takata said a 25-basis-point move is not set in stone and consecutive hikes are possible. Speculation that GPIF may adjust asset allocations added support, alongside month-end portfolio rebalancing flows.
日元在周一伦敦交易时段一度上涨1.4%至154.06，为2月以来最强水平，并超过此前日美联合干预后创下的高点。跌破155具有关键技术意义——该水平在历次干预后曾充当底部，失守触发大量止损订单连锁反应，并迫使期权做市商抛售美元。日元五个交易日累计升值约3.3%。日央行审议委员高田创表示加息25个基点“并非板上钉钉”，连续加息亦有可能。有关GPIF可能调整资产配置的猜测，以及月初的组合再平衡资金流，也对日元形成支撑。
