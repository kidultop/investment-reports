Anxious investors hope for clarity on Warsh's Fed plan at Jackson Hole
焦虑的投资者期待沃什在杰克逊霍尔阐明美联储思路
Investors this week will watch the annual Jackson Hole symposium for clues on how policymakers plan to navigate a landscape in which higher Treasury yields could do some of the tightening work for them. Few expect President Trump's new Chair Kevin Warsh to honor the forward-guidance tradition in his debut speech on Friday. They hope the Fed chair will use his speech to fully explain his roadmap for returning inflation to target and what he sees as the bond market's role in that strategy. The picture has been complicated further by the Treasury's interventionist pivot after this month's long-dated Treasury bond selloff drove yields to near two-decade highs.
投资者本周将关注杰克逊霍尔年会上政策制定者如何应对更高美债收益率可能替代加息的新格局。几乎无人预期特朗普新任命的美联储主席凯文·沃什将在周五首次演讲中承袭前瞻指引传统。他们希望美联储主席能完整阐述其将通胀拉回2%目标的路线图，以及他对债市在其中所扮演角色的看法。本月长债抛售将收益率推向近二十年高位后，财政部的干预转向进一步复杂化了这一局面。

What to expect from Fed Chair Warsh in Jackson Hole
杰克逊霍尔前瞻：市场期待美联储主席沃什说什么
Kevin Warsh does not think this is a moment for forward guidance. He thinks that's more related to crises. So he's not going to come out and say, 'We're raising rates in September' or 'We're not raising rates in September.' What they are looking for is a little more detail on how he views the risks facing the economy. What's his outlook for how these risks are going to develop, and what are the competing policy responses should one or the other develop? They also want an acknowledgment that to get the economy back to 2% inflation, an interest rate hike may be necessary.
凯文·沃什认为此时并非引入前瞻指引的时机——他认为那更适合危机情境。因此他不会直接说「我们9月要加息」或「9月不加息」。市场期待的是更多细节：他对经济面临风险如何评估？这些风险将如何演变？以及应对不同情景时有哪些政策选项？投资者还希望听到某种程度的承认——要让通胀回到2%目标，利率上调或许是必要的。

Key Takeaways From Nvidia's Earnings — WSJ
华尔街日报：英伟达财报五大要点
Revenue growth: CFO Colette Kress said to expect 70% revenue growth in Nvidia's 2028 fiscal year, significantly better than the 45% increase analysts predicted. Nvidia earnings: Chipmaker forecasts quarterly revenue at $108 billion, well above analysts' estimates
英伟达财报：Q3营收指引1080亿美元 远超华尔街预期
Nvidia forecast quarterly revenue above Wall Street estimates on Wednesday, a sign that big technology companies are still spending heavily on its chips to power their AI ambitions. The company expects third-quarter revenue of $108.0 billion, plus or minus 2%, compared with analysts' average estimate of $104.19 billion. Results from Nvidia, one of the world's most valuable firms, are considered a bellwether for the AI market. The report comes weeks after companies including Microsoft and Meta reinforced expectations that Big Tech would spend more than $730 billion on AI infrastructure this year, up from last year's $400 billion outlay.
英伟达周三给出的季度营收指引高于华尔街预期，显示大型科技公司仍在大量采购其AI芯片。公司预计第三季度营收为1080亿美元（±2%），而分析师平均预期为1041.9亿美元。作为全球市值最高的科技公司之一，英伟达业绩被视为AI市场风向标。该财报发布于微软、Meta重申大型科技公司今年AI基础设施支出将超7300亿美元（去年仅4000亿）之后仅数周。
