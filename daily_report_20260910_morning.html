Wall Street falls for a third day as bond yields hit a three-year high
美股三连跌，国债收益率创三年新高
All three major U.S. indexes fell for a third consecutive session, with the Dow Jones Industrial Average down 0.77% at 52,380.66, the S&P 500 losing 0.48% to 7,636.36 and the Nasdaq Composite falling 0.64% to 26,253.34. ECB expected to raise rates as traders price two hikes this year
欧央行今日料加息，交易员已定价年内两次
The European Central Bank announces its decision on Thursday with markets widely expecting a quarter-point rise in the deposit rate to 2.50%, after June's increase took it to 2.25%. Euro zone government bond yields reached fresh multi-year highs on the eve of the meeting as traders priced in two hikes in 2026 and a policy rate of 3.1% by late 2027. UBS managing director Kiran Ganesh said higher oil prices mean higher inflation expectations, which means a greater chance of rate increases and higher bond yields, adding there is a risk the ECB overreacts to the move in oil and causes a more negative impact on the rest of the economy. The euro rose to a more than one-week high of $1.16493.
欧洲央行将于周四公布利率决议，市场普遍预期存款利率上调25个基点至2.50%（6月加息后为2.25%）。会议前夕欧元区政府债券收益率创下多年新高，交易员已定价2026年两次加息、2027年底政策利率达到3.1%。瑞银董事总经理Kiran Ganesh表示，油价上涨意味着通胀预期升高，进而意味着加息可能性与债券收益率上升；他补充称，存在欧央行对油价波动反应过度、从而对经济其他部分造成更负面冲击的风险。欧元升至1.16493美元的一周多高位。

Yen holds near a seven-month high as BOJ tightening bets build
日元企稳于七个月高位附近，日央行加息押注升温
The yen strengthened toward the nearly seven-month high touched against the dollar in the previous session, trading around 153.35 after reaching 152.89, as traders exited short positions in the Japanese currency. It has surged about 4% over the last five sessions, with hawkish comments from Bank of Japan officials initiating a move that then snowballed as breaks of key levels triggered additional buying, market players said. Brent crude tops $100 for the first time since July as supply fears mount
布伦特原油7月来首次突破100美元，供应担忧加剧
Brent crude futures surged past $100 a barrel, breaching the symbolic level for the first time since July 24, and settled at $101.21, up $3.29 or 3.36%, after attacks on vessels in the Gulf raised worries about energy supplies. U.S. crude finished at $96.05, up 3.25%. U.S. diesel prices hit a record high last week as a global supply crunch intensified, and gasoline is at its highest ever level for the holiday period. Societe Generale strategist Manish Kabra said $100 is a round number but the break-even point for developed markets is much higher, arguing crude needs to reach $150 to derail the demand cycle, while warning that if refining margins do not decline, higher diesel prices will trickle down into services inflation.
布伦特原油期货升破每桶100美元，为7月24日以来首次突破这一象征性关口，并结算于101.21美元，上涨3.29美元（+3.36%），此前海湾地区船只遇袭引发能源供应担忧。美国原油收于96.05美元，上涨3.25%。全球供应紧张加剧，美国柴油价格上周创下历史新高，汽油则处于假期时段有记录以来最高水平。法国兴业银行策略师Manish Kabra表示，100美元只是整数关口，发达市场的油价盈亏平衡点要高得多，原油需升至150美元才会破坏需求周期；但他警告，若炼油利润不下降，柴油涨价将向下传导至服务业通胀。

🔴油价破100 + 美债收益率创三年高 = 双重压缩估值。布伦特7月24日来首破100美元（结算101.21，+3.36%），美国柴油上周创历史新高、汽油处假期时段有记录以来最高。10Y 升至4.837%（2023年11月来最高），触发点是财政部宣布回购至多60亿美元10-20年期国债——高于此前暗示的40亿，却低于华尔街预期的70-80亿，「利好出尽」反成利空。瑞银 Kiran Ganesh 一语道破传导链：**油价↑ → 通胀预期↑ → 加息概率↑ → 债券收益率↑ → 股票承压**。这是昨日欧股暴跌（STOXX 600 -1.4%，两个月最大）与美股三连跌的共同根因。
