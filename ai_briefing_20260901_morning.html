arXiv 2608.27899 OpenStamp：仅修改 unembedding 层的水印方案
arXiv 2608.27899 OpenStamp: watermark via unembedding-only edits

arXiv 2608.27899（COLM 2026 接收）提出 OpenStamp：一种面向开源权重大语言模型的水印方案，仅修改模型的 unembedding 层即可在不改写主网络权重的前提下，对生成文本插入可被统计检测的指纹信号。论文显示在 Llama-3、Qwen3、Mistral 三个系列上，对 perplexity 的影响均低于 0.3%。

检测端为 z-test，开源模型权重可被任意方托管，发布者与第三方可分别独立验证水印存在。论文作者来自 University of Maryland 与 Cornell Tech。

arXiv 2608.27899 (accepted at COLM 2026) introduces OpenStamp: a watermark scheme for open-weight LLMs that inserts a statistically detectable fingerprint by editing only the unembedding layer — leaving the rest of the network unchanged. Reported perplexity impact is <0.3% on Llama-3, Qwen3, and Mistral families.

Detection is a z-test; open weights allow both publisher and third parties to independently verify watermark presence. Authors are from the University of Maryland and Cornell Tech.

WHY IT MATTERS · 为何重要
当开源厂商开始主动水印（如 Hugging Face、Vulcan 等社区），下游微调者事实上陷入"看不见约束"的局面。OpenStamp 这种"只动最后一层"的设计为未来 LICENSE 显式要求的水印机制提供了非侵入式的工程模板。
If open-source distributors start to watermark by default (HF, Vulcan-style communities), downstream fine-tuners will face "invisible constraints". OpenStamp's "touch only the last layer" design is a non-intrusive engineering template for future license-mandated watermarking.
