头条 Research
8 月 6 日，Google DeepMind 在《自然》杂志发表论文，宣布开源天气预报模型 WeatherNext。2025 年 10 月，WeatherNext 在飓风 Melissa 仍为一级时就以 80% 置信度预测其将升级为五级飓风并袭击牙买加——五天后果然应验。这是美国国家飓风中心历史上首次有模型在风暴仍为一级时预测其将升至五级。模型平均比现有预报多一天提前量，三天预报准确度相当于现有模型的两天。值得注意的是，WeatherNext 使用相对较低分辨率的大气数据即实现了强度预测突破。模型已开源供研究人员使用和改进。
On August 6, Google DeepMind published a Nature paper and open-sourced WeatherNext, its weather forecasting model. In October 2025, WeatherNext predicted with 80% confidence that Hurricane Melissa would intensify from Category 1 to Category 5 and strike Jamaica — five days before it did. This was the first time any model predicted a storm would reach Cat 5 while still at Cat 1. The model averages one extra day of lead time, with 3-day forecast accuracy matching conventional 2-day forecasts. Notably, it achieved intensity prediction breakthroughs using relatively low-resolution atmospheric data. The model is now open-sourced.

The SafeKeep paper (arXiv, July 31) finds that the way tool schemas are written for AI agents inherently weakens models' ability to refuse harmful requests. Switching safety checks from structured schemas to flat text specifications raised harmful request refusal rates from 23.8% to 70.6% and reduced prompt injection success from 25.6% to 2.5%. The research reveals an overlooked security layer in the MCP/Function Calling ecosystem — developers' formatting choices in tool definitions directly affect model safety behavior.
为何重要 Why it matters：随着 MCP 协议被广泛采用，工具 schema 安全成为 Agent 生态中不可忽视的攻击面。该研究提供了可操作的缓解方案，对 Agent 安全工程有直接实践价值。 / As MCP protocols see widespread adoption, tool schema security becomes a non-trivial attack surface in the Agent ecosystem.
