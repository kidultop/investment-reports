Wall Street indexes slip as oil prices rise, retail results awaited
美股三大指数收低，油价上涨且市场等待零售商业绩
Wall Street's three major indexes finished lower on Monday (S&P 500 -0.52% to 7,745.06, Dow -0.51% to 53,459.78, Nasdaq -0.31% to 26,644.91) as oil prices rose on US-Iran stalemate. Microsoft and Meta were the biggest drags, both down over 3%; chip stocks rallied with the PHLX semiconductor index up 1.6% (Micron +4%, Applied Materials +5.5%). Energy was the sole gainer among S&P sectors (+0.87%). Investors await Home Depot (Tue) and Walmart (Thu) results for consumer clues.
周一美股三大指数集体收低：标普500跌0.52%至7745.06、道指跌0.51%至53459.78、纳指跌0.31%至26644.91，因美伊僵局推升油价。微软与Meta领跌，均跌超3%；芯片股逆势走强，费城半导体指数涨1.6%（美光+4%、应用材料+5.5%）。能源为标普唯一上涨板块（+0.87%）。市场等待家得宝（周二）与沃尔玛（周四）财报以研判消费。

Oil settles up over $2 as Iran war stalemate stokes supply concerns
美伊僵局加剧供应担忧，油价收涨逾2美元
Oil prices settled up more than $2 a barrel on Monday as investor pessimism about diplomatic efforts to resolve the Iran war fanned global supply worries. WTI rose about 3% to ~$84.95 and Brent to ~$90.87. The US-Iran 60-day ceasefire memorandum expired with no extension; Trump demanded Iran's surrender and threatened to bomb Oman if it hindered a deal. Hormuz Strait shipping slowed sharply, with a UAE tanker seized near the waterway, stoking fears one-fifth of seaborne crude flows are at risk.
周一油价收涨逾2美元，因市场对美国与伊朗外交努力解决战争的前景悲观，供应担忧升温。WTI涨约3%至约84.95美元、布伦特至约90.87美元。美伊60天停火备忘录到期且未延长；特朗普要求伊朗投降并威胁若阿曼阻碍协议将轰炸该国。霍尔木兹海峡航运量骤降，一艘阿联酋油轮在海峡附近被扣押，全球约五分之一海运原油运输面临风险。
reuters.com⭐⭐⭐⭐
Saudi Aramco offers crude outside Hormuz to some Asian refiners
沙特阿美绕开霍尔木兹向部分亚洲炼厂直供原油
Saudi Aramco is offering crude oil outside the Strait of Hormuz to some Asian refiners through private negotiations, two sources said on Monday, in a strategy similar to that adopted by Abu Dhabi National Oil Co. The move reflects producers' efforts to route supply around the disrupted waterway as Hormuz shipping volumes have fallen to a fraction of pre-war levels, keeping a geopolitical risk premium in oil prices.
据两位知情人士周一称，沙特阿美正通过私下谈判向部分亚洲炼厂提供绕开霍尔木兹海峡的原油，策略类似阿布扎比国家石油公司(ADNOC)。这反映产油国在霍尔木兹航运量降至战前一小部分之际，努力为供应另辟通道，使油价中的地缘政治风险溢价得以维持。

Trading Day: Bonds play the blues as long-dated yields hit decades-high
债市忧郁：长端收益率升至数十年高位，拖累股市
Long-dated government bond yields around the world rose on Monday to levels not seen in decades, weighing on stocks which were also pressured by deepening skepticism around a US-Iran peace deal and oil prices rising to a six-week high. The US 30-year Treasury yield climbed above 5.30%, its highest since 2007, while the 10-year rose to ~4.72%. The Federal Reserve's rate path remains uncertain ahead of Wednesday's FOMC minutes.
周一全球长端国债收益率升至数十年来未见的高位，拖累股市；与此同时，市场对美伊和平协议前景的疑虑加深、油价升至六周高点也构成压力。美国30年期国债收益率升破5.30%（2007年以来最高），10年期升至约4.72%。美联储利率路径在周三FOMC纪要公布前仍不明朗。

Nvidia to provide up to $105 billion guarantee for OpenAI's Ohio data center
英伟达为OpenAI俄亥俄数据中心提供最高1050亿美元担保
Nvidia agreed to provide a guarantee of up to $105 billion to help OpenAI lease a sprawling 8-gigawatt data center in Ohio developed by SoftBank-owned SB Energy, and will invest $1.5 billion in SB Energy. Huang said the deal is not circular financing. Nvidia could make up to $600 billion in revenue from OpenAI by 2030 by selling 16 GW of computing power. The move deepens Nvidia's strategy of financing the infrastructure built around its chips.
英伟达同意提供最高1050亿美元担保，协助OpenAI租赁由软银旗下SB Energy在俄亥俄州开发的8吉瓦大型数据中心，并将向SB Energy投资15亿美元。黄仁勋称该交易并非循环融资。英伟达到2030年通过向OpenAI出售16吉瓦算力有望获得最高6000亿美元收入。此举深化了英伟达为其芯片配套基础设施融资的战略。
