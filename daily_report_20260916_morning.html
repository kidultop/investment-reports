Global shares fall as Treasury yields scale fresh peaks, Fed decision looms
全球股市下跌，美债收益率创2007年来新高，美联储决议在即
Global stocks were lower on Tuesday, continuing a selloff from the previous session, after U.S. Treasury yields hit their highest level since 2007 and with oil prices firmly above $100 per barrel. Investors' attention was turning to the Federal Reserve, with traders anticipating at least a quarter-point interest rate hike. The yield on benchmark U.S. 10-year notes rose 3.47 basis points to 4.996%. German Bund yields rose to their highest in over 17 years at 3.56%. Consumer discretionary, communication services and utilities drove losses while energy shares were the biggest gainers. The Bank of Japan is widely expected to raise its rate by 25 bps to 1.25% on Friday.
周二全球股市继续下跌，因美国国债收益率升至2007年以来最高水平，油价站稳100美元上方。市场焦点转向美联储，交易员普遍预期至少加息25个基点。基准10年期美债收益率上涨3.47个基点至4.996%，德国国债收益率升至逾17年高位的3.56%。非必需消费品、通信服务和公用事业板块领跌，能源股涨幅最大。日本央行周五预计加息25个基点至1.25%。

10-year Treasury yield crosses 5% for first time since 2007 ahead of Fed decision
10年期美债收益率2007年来首破5%，美联储决议前市场紧绷
US stock futures fell on Tuesday as investors remained cautious ahead of the Federal Reserve's policy decision, while surging Treasury yields and elevated crude oil prices fuelled expectations of the first rate hike in more than three years. The benchmark 10-year Treasury yield crossed the 5% threshold, reaching 5.041%, its highest level since 2007. Traders were pricing around a 90% chance of a quarter-percentage-point increase on Wednesday. Chipmakers were trading in a narrow range while Nvidia edged marginally higher, and crude prices remained elevated as Iran-backed Houthi attacks deepened energy supply concerns.
周二美股期货下跌，投资者在美联储政策决议前保持谨慎，飙升的国债收益率和高企的原油价格强化了三年多来首次加息的预期。基准10年期美债收益率突破5%关口至5.041%，为2007年以来最高。交易员押注周三加息25个基点的概率约90%。芯片股窄幅波动，英伟达小幅走高；胡塞武装袭击加剧能源供应担忧，原油价格维持高位。
reuters.com⭐⭐⭐⭐
Morgan Stanley turns hawkish, sees two Fed rate hikes in 2026 and ECB hike in December
摩根士丹利转鹰：预计美联储年内两次加息，欧央行12月再加息
Morgan Stanley expects the Federal Reserve to raise its benchmark interest rate by 25 basis points at its September 15-16 meeting and deliver another quarter-point increase in December, citing persistent inflation and oil prices above $100 a barrel. The brokerage pointed to potential second-round effects from higher energy prices, strong AI-driven demand and concerns over Fed policy credibility. It also expects the ECB to raise its deposit rate by another 25 basis points in December to 2.75%, reversing its earlier view that the ECB tightening cycle had ended.
摩根士丹利预计美联储将在9月15-16日会议加息25个基点，并在12月再加息25个基点，理由包括通胀持续、油价突破100美元。该行指出能源价格的二次效应、AI驱动的强劲需求，以及对美联储政策信誉的担忧。同时预计欧洲央行12月将存款利率再上调25个基点至2.75%，扭转此前认为欧央行紧缩周期已结束的看法。

Oil jumps nearly $3 as Saudi export halt, Libya outages stoke supply fears
沙特出口中断叠加利比亚停产，油价大涨近3美元
Oil prices rose nearly $3 on Tuesday after a report said oil loadings at Saudi Arabia's Red Sea port of Yanbu have been suspended, and Libya halted operations at three oil fields. Brent crude futures were up $2.81, or 2.66%, at $108.49 a barrel, while U.S. WTI futures were up $3.29 at $104.68. Commodity vessel traffic through the Strait of Hormuz dropped to four on Monday, down from 10 a day earlier. U.S. diesel futures rose more than 5.9%, on track for a record close, as half of Russia's six top diesel refineries cut output.
周二油价上涨近3美元，此前有报道称沙特红海延布港石油装运已暂停，利比亚三个油田停产。布伦特原油期货上涨2.81美元或2.66%至每桶108.49美元，WTI原油期货上涨3.29美元至104.68美元。霍尔木兹海峡商品船通行量周一降至4艘，此前一日为10艘。美国柴油期货涨超5.9%，有望创纪录收盘，因俄罗斯六大柴油炼厂中有一半减产。
reuters.com⭐⭐⭐
Goldman warns Brent could top $120 as Saudi pipeline repair seen taking up to eight weeks
高盛警告布伦特或突破120美元，沙特管道维修最长需8周
Saudi Arabia could exhaust crude available for export within days unless the East-West Pipeline resumes operations, threatening up to 4% of global oil supply. Goldman Sachs said the recent attack may be more severe and could threaten the remaining 2 million barrels per day of Yanbu exports, with repair assessments ranging from 'very soon' to eight weeks. The attacks marked a meaningful escalation and increased the probability of Brent rising above $120 a barrel. Capital Economics said several weeks of closure could lift Brent towards $130 per barrel absent greater flows through the Strait of Hormuz.
除非东西输油管道恢复运行，沙特可能在数日内耗尽可出口原油，威胁全球最多4%的石油供应。高盛表示此次袭击可能更为严重，或威胁延布港剩余的每日200万桶出口，维修评估从「很快」到8周不等。袭击标志着冲突显著升级，布伦特突破120美元的概率上升。凯投宏观称若管道关闭数周且霍尔木兹海峡流量未增，布伦特可能冲向130美元。

Houthis strike Saudi airbase as Gulf states postpone Iran talks on Hormuz shipping
胡塞袭击沙特空军基地，海湾国家推迟伊朗霍尔木兹会谈
Iran-backed Houthi forces fired dozens of missiles and drones at a military airbase in Khamis Mushait in southern Saudi Arabia, targeting aircraft hangars, radar systems, runways and ammunition depots in retaliation for Saudi airstrikes in Yemen. Gulf Arab states postponed planned discussions with Iran on the Strait of Hormuz. Commodity vessel traffic through Hormuz dropped to fewer than 10 transits a day over the weekend from a 10-day average of 14. Oman's Maritime Security Centre said the Panama-flagged tanker El Gaia was being towed to port after a fire in its engine room following an attack.
伊朗支持的胡塞武装向沙特南部海米斯穆谢特军事空军基地发射数十枚导弹和无人机，袭击机库、雷达系统、跑道和弹药库，以报复沙特对也门的空袭。海湾阿拉伯国家推迟了与伊朗关于霍尔木兹海峡的会谈。周末霍尔木兹海峡商品船通行量降至每日不足10艘，此前10日均值为14艘。阿曼海上安全中心称巴拿马籍油轮El Gaia在遇袭后机舱起火，正被拖往港口。
