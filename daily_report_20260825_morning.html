Trump admin unveils anti-Iran global sanctions plan, signals China not exempt
特朗普政府推出对伊全球制裁计划,示意中国不豁免
The Trump administration unveiled a plan to isolate Iran's economy dubbed 'Operation Economic Outcast.' Treasury Secretary Scott Bessent said at the Treasury Department: 'No one is above the reach of U.S. sanctions... If they facilitate transactions and are part of the ecosystem that turns Iranian oil into money, into repression, they will be targeted.' The plan targets those who buy or transport Iranian oil, facilitate financial transactions, accept flights and allow seaborne transfers, plus Iran's digital assets, technology, gold, aviation and shipping sectors.
特朗普政府周一提议计划,威胁对维持伊朗经济运转的「协助者」实施二级制裁,代号「Operation Economic Outcast」(经济放逐行动)。财政部长贝森特在财政部宣布:「任何人都不会凌驾于美国制裁之上...如果他们参与交易,是把伊朗石油变成资金、变成镇压的生态系统的一部分,他们就会成为目标。」计划目标是购买或运输伊朗石油、协助金融交易、接受航班及海上转运的实体,以及扩大至伊朗数字资产、科技、黄金、航空、航运行业。

Trump says U.S. will hike Canada auto tariffs to 50% as trade war escalates
特朗普:美国将把对加拿大汽车关税提高至50%,贸易战升级
President Donald Trump said the U.S. will raise tariffs on imports of cars, trucks and auto parts from Canada to 50% on Jan. 1, 2027. Trump's latest tariff threat would double top-line U.S. tariffs on Canadian auto imports, which currently sit at 25%. 特朗普总统周一表示,美国将从2027年1月1日起将加拿大进口的汽车、卡车和汽车零部件的关税提高至50%。特朗普最新的关税威胁将使美国对加拿大汽车进口税翻倍,目前为25%。加拿大原谋求降低这些关税作为与美国新贸易协议的一部分,但该协议上周破裂。

Bond Yields Rise Despite Treasury Efforts to Curb Borrowing Costs
尽管财政部努力抑制借贷成本,债券收益率仍在上升
