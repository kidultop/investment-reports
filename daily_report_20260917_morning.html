BOJ set to raise policy rate to 1.25% at September meeting
日本央行料将在9月会议上将政策利率上调至1.25%
The Bank of Japan's board is widely expected to raise the target for the overnight interest rate to 1.25% from 1% at its Sept. 17-18 meeting. It would be the bank's sixth rate hike in the current cycle. Markets are pricing in near certainty of a BOJ hike as elevated energy prices from the Iran war could push underlying inflation above 2%.
日本央行董事会普遍预期将在9月17-18日会议上将隔夜利率目标从1%上调至1.25%。这将是本轮周期的第六次加息。市场几乎确定日本央行将加息，因为伊朗战争导致的能源价格高企可能将潜在通胀推升至2%以上。
