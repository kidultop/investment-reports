The 10-year US Treasury note yield traded near the key 5% mark after the Federal Reserve rate increase, with Chairman Kevin Warsh highlighting persistent inflation risks. The hot inflation data has pressured the long end of the curve in recent weeks.
美联储加息后，10年期美债收益率接近关键5%关口，主席沃什强调持续通胀风险。基准收益率约5.008%，2年期上升5基点至4.717%。高盛资产管理指出，美联储暗示不设想激进紧缩周期，12月再加息一次为基本情景。近期火热的通胀数据已施压长端收益率曲线。

Fed Hikes Rates 25bp to 3.75-4.00% in Unanimous Vote, First Since July 2023; Warsh: 'Inflation Too High for Too Long'
美联储全票加息25基点至3.75%-4.00%，为2023年7月以来首次；沃什：通胀过高且持续太久
The Federal Reserve raised its key interest rate by 25 basis points to a range of 3.75%-4.00% on September 16, 2026, the first hike in over three years. The decision was unanimous (12-0). Fed Chair Kevin Warsh said the Fed's predominant focus remains on price stability, stating 'inflation is too high and has been for too long.' The SEP dot plot showed 16 of 18 officials expect at least one more hike this year, with the median year-end rate forecast rising to 4.1%. Stocks whipsawed lower after the announcement, with the Dow falling 631 points.
美联储9月16日加息25基点至3.75%-4.00%，为逾三年首次。决议获12票全票通过。主席沃什重申通胀过高且持续太久。点阵图显示18名官员中16人预计年内至少再加息一次，年底利率中位数升至4.1%。美股决议后跳水，道指跌631点。

The statement noted 'today's policy action will support a timelier return to the Committee's 2 percent goal.'
美联储9月经济预测摘要（SEP）显示鹰派转向。2026年底联邦基金利率中位数预测从6月的3.8%上调至4.1%，暗示年内还有一次25基点加息。18名FOMC参与者中16人预计至少再加息一次。美联储还将2026年PCE通胀预期上调至3.7%。

IBM子公司Anderon敲定10亿美元CHIPS奖励，建设美国量子晶圆厂
