Dollar holds firm as Middle East hostilities lift oil

The dollar held firm on Wednesday as renewed hostilities in the Middle East pushed oil prices higher and revived inflation concerns, with its safe-haven appeal reinforced by rising Treasury yields and growing expectations of a policy tightening this month. Global bond yields climb to highest since 2008 crisis as debt deluge spooks investors
债务洪流冲击投资者，全球债券收益率升至2008年危机以来最高
Global bond yields climbed to their highest levels since the 2008 financial crisis as rising oil prices, persistent inflation concerns and mounting government debt pushed investors to demand higher returns on government bonds. The yield on Bloomberg's gauge of global sovereign bonds rose for a fourth consecutive session to 3.72%, the highest since mid-2008. The 10-year Japanese government bond yield touched 3% for the first time since 1996, 30-year British yields reached their highest since 1998 and the 10-year Treasury climbed to 4.8%, its highest since early 2025. The global bond market was worth roughly $109 trillion according to the OECD, with governments and companies expected to borrow a record $29 trillion in 2026. The move accelerated after Fed Chairman Kevin Warsh reiterated his commitment to bringing inflation under control at Jackson Hole, warning the Fed would 'have work to do' if underlying inflation is not returning to the 2% target.
在油价上涨、通胀担忧持续和政府债务攀升的推动下，投资者要求更高的政府债券回报，全球债券收益率升至2008年金融危机以来最高水平。彭博全球主权债券收益率指标连续第四个交易日上行至3.72%，为2008年中以来最高。日本10年期国债收益率自1996年以来首次触及3%，英国30年期收益率升至1998年以来最高，美国10年期国债收益率升至4.8%，为2025年初以来最高。经合组织数据显示全球债券市场规模约109万亿美元，2026年政府与企业借款规模预计达创纪录的29万亿美元。美联储主席沃什在杰克逊霍尔重申控制通胀的决心，并警告若核心通胀未回归2%目标，美联储'还有工作要做'，加速了这一走势。

Broadcom raises AI chip forecast as Big Tech keeps writing bigger checks
博通上调AI芯片指引，大型科技公司持续加大投入
Broadcom on Wednesday forecast strong AI chip sales for the next two years, offering fresh evidence that Big Tech's appetite for AI infrastructure remains undiminished. The company reported third-quarter revenue of $29.6 billion, up 86% year on year, with AI chip sales of $16.7 billion, up 221%, and semiconductor solutions revenue of $20.8 billion, or 70% of total revenue. Google defeats US bid to force ad tech sale
谷歌击败美国强制出售广告技术业务的诉求
Alphabet's Google escaped a breakup of its advertising technology business on Wednesday, when a judge in Virginia rejected U.S. antitrust enforcers' bid to force a sale of Google's online advertising exchange. U.S. Judge Leonie Brinkema in Alexandria declined to make Google sell AdX, where publishers pay Google a 20% fee to sell ads in auctions that happen instantly when users load websites, and accepted most of the parties' proposed behavioral remedies. The ruling is the third time in a row that a judge has rejected a bid by U.S. antitrust enforcers to break up Big Tech, following the failed attempts to force Meta to sell Instagram and WhatsApp and to make Google sell its Chrome browser. The DOJ and a coalition of states sued Google in 2023 over dominance in advertising technology used by online publishers and websites.
Alphabet 旗下谷歌周三躲过了广告技术业务被拆分的命运，弗吉尼亚州一名法官拒绝了美国反垄断执法机构强制出售其在线广告交易所的要求。亚历山德里亚的美国法官 Leonie Brinkema 拒绝要求谷歌出售 AdX（出版商需向谷歌支付20%费用，在用户加载网页的瞬时竞价中出售广告），并接受了双方提出的大部分行为性救济措施。这是美国法官连续第三次驳回拆分大型科技公司的诉求，此前强制 Meta 出售 Instagram 与 WhatsApp、强制谷歌出售 Chrome 浏览器的尝试均已失败。司法部与多州组成的联盟于2023年就谷歌在出版商与网站所用广告技术市场的支配地位提起诉讼。

US private payroll growth slows in August; factory orders rebound in July
美国8月民间就业增速放缓，7月工厂订单反弹
U.S. private payrolls increased less than expected in August as strength in education and health services was offset by job losses in manufacturing and a few other industries. Private employment rose by 38,000 jobs last month after an upwardly revised 46,000 in July, according to the ADP National Employment Report, against economists' forecast of 48,000. Education and health services added 45,000 positions, leisure and hospitality 16,000 and construction 12,000, while manufacturing shed 17,000 and professional and business services lost 16,000. Large firms with at least 500 employees accounted for 34,000 of the gains, or 89% of the net increase, leaving small businesses with only 3,000. Pay growth was steady, with job-stayers' base pay up 3.0% year on year. Separately, factory orders rose 0.9% in July, led by a 12.7% surge in civilian aircraft orders, though core capital goods orders excluding aircraft were unchanged rather than up 0.2% as previously reported. Nonfarm payrolls on Friday are expected to rise by 56,000 with the unemployment rate steady at 4.1%.
美国8月民间就业增幅不及预期，教育与医疗服务业的强劲表现被制造业及其他若干行业的岗位流失抵消。ADP 全国就业报告显示，上月民间就业增加3.8万人，7月上修后为4.6万人，而经济学家预期为4.8万。教育与医疗服务增加4.5万个岗位，休闲与住宿1.6万，建筑业1.2万；制造业减少1.7万，专业与商业服务减少1.6万。员工数至少500人的大企业贡献了3.4万个岗位（占净增的89%），小企业仅增3,000个。薪资增长稳定，留任员工基本薪资同比升3.0%。另外，7月工厂订单增长0.9%，受民用飞机订单激增12.7%拉动，但剔除飞机的核心资本品订单为零增长（此前报为+0.2%）。周五非农就业预计增加5.6万，失业率维持4.1%。
