Shares skid in Asia as oil, yields stay high
油价与收益率居高不下，亚太股市周一承压下滑
Share markets slipped on Monday in Asia as fresh fighting broke out between the United States and Iran, lifting oil prices, while bond yields stayed elevated after investors narrowed the odds on a U.S. rate hike. Brent futures climbed 1.4% to $89.38 a barrel after U.S. forces struck two of Iran's launchers on its island of Larak on Sunday; in response Iran was attacking U.S. forces stationed in Jordan. Markets lifted the probability of a September rate increase to 57%, shoving short-term Treasury yields sharply higher and flattening the curve. Japan's Nikkei fell 2.1% and South Korean stocks fell 2.4%, while two-year Treasury yields held at 4.36% after jumping almost 12 basis points on Friday.
周一亚太股市下滑，美伊冲突重燃推高油价，同时投资者收窄对美国加息的押注令债券收益率持续高企。继美军周日打击伊朗拉拉克岛两处发射装置后，布伦特原油期货上涨1.4%至每桶89.38美元，伊朗随即袭击驻约旦美军作为报复。市场将9月加息概率推升至57%，短端美债收益率大幅走高、收益率曲线趋平。日经指数下跌2.1%，韩国股市下跌2.4%；两年期美债收益率维持在4.36%，周五曾跳升近12个基点。

Bessent expects Japan to take action to boost yen, signals BOJ rate-hike chance
贝森特预期日本将采取行动提振日元，暗示日银9月加息可能性
U.S. Treasury Secretary Scott Bessent said he believes Japan's government and central bank will take action that leads to a stronger yen, signaling a strong chance of a Bank of Japan interest rate hike in September. He told Reuters he expects BOJ Governor Kazuo Ueda to do the right thing on monetary policy to combat yen declines, and told CNBC he has information that the market doesn't have. When asked whether that meant raising interest rates, Bessent said he thinks the market's pricing that in now. The dollar stood at 159.73 yen on Monday, still close to the 160 mark seen as heightening the chance of yen-buying intervention. Sources told Reuters the BOJ is set to raise rates as soon as its September 17-18 meeting.
美国财政部长贝森特表示，他相信日本政府和央行将采取推动日元走强的行动，暗示日本央行9月加息的可能性很大。他对路透表示，预计日本央行行长植田和男会在货币政策上「做正确的事」以应对日元贬值，并对CNBC称自己「掌握了市场没有的信息」。当被问及这是否意味着加息时，贝森特称「我认为市场现在已经把这一点计入价格了」。周一美元兑日元报159.73，仍接近被视为可能触发买入日元干预的160关口。消息人士向路透透露，日本央行最早可能在9月17–18日的会议上加息。

Trading Day: Bonds shaken, and stirred
交易日复盘：债市被震动
The benchmark 10-year U.S. Treasury yield on Monday spiked above 4.75% for the first time since January last year, part of a worldwide bond selloff that weighed on stock markets as investors digested an escalation in the U.S.-Iran war and oil rising back above $90 a barrel. Wall Street closed in the red with the Dow down 0.7%, the S&P 500 off 0.3% and the Nasdaq 0.1% lower; nine S&P sectors fell with industrials and utilities both down 1.2%, while energy gained 2.1%. CrowdStrike, Tesla and SanDisk all rose 5% or more. France's 10- and 30-year yields hit their highest since 2008, Germany's 30-year its highest since 2011, and Japan's two- and 10-year yields rose to their highest since the mid-1990s.
周一美国10年期基准国债收益率突破4.75%，为去年1月以来首次，这是全球债市抛售的一部分——投资者正在消化美伊战争升级与油价重回90美元上方的影响，股市承压。华尔街收盘下跌：道指跌0.7%、标普500跌0.3%、纳指跌0.1%；标普11个板块中有9个下跌，工业与公用事业均跌1.2%，能源板块涨2.1%。CrowdStrike、特斯拉与闪迪涨幅均达5%以上。法国10年期与30年期收益率创2008年以来新高，德国30年期创2011年以来新高，日本2年期与10年期收益率升至1990年代中期以来最高。

Why the Sandisk post-earnings sell-off looks absurd
闪迪财报后的抛售为何显得荒谬
In the rush to exit Sandisk after a slight guidance miss on Wednesday night, investors may be overlooking a blunt reality: the company is executing well as the AI boom keeps up demand for its memory chips. Sandisk closed out its fiscal year with $20.2 billion in sales, up from $7.4 billion a year ago, a 175% jump. It reported blowout fiscal fourth quarter results, comfortably beating estimates with non-GAAP earnings of $39.25 per share on $8.97 billion in revenue, while data center revenue rose 103% sequentially to $2.98 billion. The stock initially tanked 10% because the midpoint of first-quarter fiscal 2027 revenue guidance of $10.3 billion to $10.8 billion came in below consensus of $10.8 billion. CEO David Goeckeler said the company now has over four years of demand visibility. Shares closed at $1,566.70, up 5.50%.
在周三晚间因指引略微不及预期而急于抛售闪迪的投资者，可能忽视了一个直白的事实：在AI热潮持续拉动存储芯片需求之际，这家公司执行得相当出色。闪迪本财年销售额达202亿美元，较上年同期的74亿美元增长175%。第四财季业绩爆表，非GAAP每股收益39.25美元、营收89.7亿美元，远超预期；数据中心营收环比增长103%至29.8亿美元。此前股价一度重挫10%，只因2027财年一季度营收指引中值103亿–108亿美元低于一致预期的108亿美元。CEO David Goeckeler 表示公司目前拥有超过四年的需求可见度。股价收于1566.70美元，上涨5.50%。
