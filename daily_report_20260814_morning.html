US Treasury signals pressure on Bank of Japan for September rate move; odds at 68%
美国财长向日本央行施压，市场定价9月加息概率升至68%
U.S. Treasury Secretary Scott Bessent stated he expects policy follow-up after coordinated yen intervention, with markets now pricing 68% odds of a 15bp rate increase at the BOJ's September 19-20 meeting, up from 52% before his statement. A September hike would mark Japan's third post-pandemic adjustment and could trigger a global unwinding of yen carry trades.

Oil drops on lower demand forecasts despite deadlock in US-Iran talks
油价因需求预期走弱下跌，霍尔木兹谈判僵局仍构成支撑
Oil prices fell more than $1 on Thursday as forecasters lowered global oil demand projections for 2026 because of disruptions from the U.S.-Israeli war on Iran, though supply constraints from the conflict kept a floor. Brent slipped toward $87.69 and WTI toward $81.97. OPEC cut its 2026 demand growth forecast to 580,000 bpd; the IEA sees a 1.6 million bpd contraction. U.S. crude inventories rose 17.4 million barrels, the largest weekly build since Jan 2023. Talks to reopen the Strait of Hormuz remained deadlocked, with attacks on shipping highlighting persistent supply risks.
油价周四跌逾1美元，布伦特跌破$88、WTI跌破$82，因OPEC与IEA下调2026年全球需求增长预期，且美国原油库存大增1740万桶（2023年1月以来最大单周增幅）。但美伊就重开霍尔木兹海峡的谈判仍陷僵局，胡塞武装袭击航运令供应风险持续，为油价提供底部支撑。
