SpaceX's first quarterly results as a public company beat expectations, but AI costs hit stock
SpaceX上市后首份季报超预期，但AI成本拖累股价
SpaceX on Tuesday posted its first quarterly results as a public company, highlighting a 92% rise in revenue on strong growth in its Starlink satellite-internet and AI businesses. Revenue rose to $7.8B from $4.1B YoY, beating $6.9B consensus. The firm lost 9 cents a share, beating 26-cent loss expected. CEO Elon Musk expects a 'significant percentage' of Nvidia's GPUs next year. Shares fell 7% in late trading after rising 9.4% in regular session. The earnings release is followed by unlock of 911M shares previously subject to restrictions.
SpaceX周二发布上市后首份季报，受Starlink卫星互联网与AI业务强劲增长驱动，营收同比增长92%。营收从去年41亿增至78亿美元，超出共识预期69亿。每股亏损0.09美元，优于预期亏损0.26美元。马斯克预计明年将获得英伟达GPU中的显著份额。股价盘后跌7%（盘前涨9.4%）。本周随之而来的还有9.11亿股限售股解禁。

AMD Reports Second Quarter 2026 Financial Results
AMD公布2026年第二季度财务业绩
AMD today announced Q2 2026 financial results. Second quarter revenue was $11.5 billion, gross margin was 54%, operating income was $2.0 billion, net income was $2.3 billion and diluted EPS was $1.38. Non-GAAP diluted EPS was $1.66.
