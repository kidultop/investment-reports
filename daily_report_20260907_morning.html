Wall Street ends lower as solid jobs data fuels hawkish Fed bets
美股收跌：强劲非农推升美联储加息押注
Wall Street dipped on Friday as a robust jobs report raised the probability that the U.S. Federal Reserve will increase its key interest rate at this month's policy meeting. The economy added 162,000 jobs in August, nearly three times the 56,000 consensus, while June and July payrolls were revised up by a combined 55,000. Unemployment held at 4.1%. Tokyo inflation accelerates again, backing Bank of Japan hike
东京通胀连续第三个月加速，支持日央行9月加息
Tokyo core CPI rose 1.8% year-on-year in August, a third straight monthly acceleration and the closest the capital has come to the BOJ's 2% target this year, matching the median estimate in a Bloomberg survey. The print strengthens the case for a hike at the BOJ's Sept 17-18 meeting, where most economists expect the policy rate to rise from 1.00% to 1.25%. Overnight indexed swaps price roughly a 98% probability of a September move. Deputy Governor Ryozo Himino said policymakers should pay greater attention to upside price risks than in the past.
东京8月核心CPI同比上涨1.8%，为连续第三个月加速，也是今年最接近日央行2%目标的水平，符合彭博调查中值。该数据强化了9月17-18日会议加息的理由，多数经济学家预计政策利率将由1.00%升至1.25%。隔夜指数掉期定价9月加息概率约98%。副行长冰见野良三表示政策制定者应比以往更关注物价上行风险。
reuters.com⭐⭐⭐
ECB set to raise rates in September, but then done, Reuters poll finds
路透调查：欧央行9月加息后或就此收手
All 65 economists in an Aug 31-Sept 3 Reuters survey predicted the ECB would lift its deposit rate by a quarter-point to 2.50% on Sept 10, up from 83% in an August poll. About 91% see the rate ending the year at 2.50% and 78% see it staying there until mid-2027. If correct, it would be the ECB's shortest hiking campaign since 2011. Euro zone inflation accelerated to 3.3% in August, driven largely by energy costs, but economists see it as a supply-side shock. Interest-rate futures, by contrast, are pricing in a third hike.
路透8月31日至9月3日调查显示，全部65位经济学家预计欧央行将在9月10日加息25个基点至2.50%（8月调查为83%）。约91%预计年底利率维持2.50%，78%认为至少持续到2027年中。若预期兑现，这将是欧央行2011年以来最短的紧缩周期。欧元区8月通胀升至3.3%，主要由能源驱动，经济学家视为供给侧冲击；但利率期货已计入第三次加息，双方存在分歧。

Nvidia strikes $12.9bn deal to buy AI platform Hugging Face
英伟达129亿美元收购AI开源平台 Hugging Face
Nvidia has agreed to buy artificial intelligence platform Hugging Face in a deal valued at about $12.9bn, one of the AI chipmaker's biggest acquisitions as it expands into software. Hugging Face is used by more than 18 million developers and hosts more than three million AI models, with over 200,000 companies on the platform. Nvidia said Hugging Face would remain open to developers and that users would not be required to use its chips or services. Nvidia will pay about $11.9bn to investors plus up to $1bn in stock incentives to employees. The deal faces antitrust scrutiny and is expected to close in the first half of 2027.
英伟达同意以约129亿美元收购AI平台Hugging Face，是其向软件领域扩张的最大收购之一。该平台拥有超1800万开发者、托管逾300万个AI模型，超过20万家企业使用。英伟达承诺平台保持开放，开发者不必使用其芯片或服务。交易结构为向投资者支付约119亿美元现金，另向加入的员工提供最高10亿美元股权激励。交易需通过多国反垄断审查，预计2027年上半年完成交割。
