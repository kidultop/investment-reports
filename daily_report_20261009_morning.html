Accounts of the ECB's September 9-10 policy meeting released on Thursday showed all policymakers agreed that inflation risks are skewed towards even higher readings given exceptional volatility in energy prices, even as a long list of officials speaking across Europe argued that underlying price trends are more benign, that second-round effects from the energy shock have so far been negligible and that longer-term inflation expectations remain anchored. DBS fell as much as 5% in its steepest drop in over a year, while OCBC and UOB each dropped 4-5%; the three banks account for more than half of the main index and over a third of the MSCI ASEAN gauge, which marked its worst day since early March. Samsung Electronics on Thursday projected a nearly nine-fold jump in third-quarter operating profit to 107.4 trillion won ($80.17 billion), the first time any technology company has guided above 100 trillion won and ahead of an LSEG SmartEstimate, as booming AI infrastructure demand outpaces memory chip supply growth — a gap expected to persist into 2027. The pace may moderate: analysts expect Q4 profit up just 8.2% sequentially, and TrendForce sees Q4 DRAM contract prices rising 10-15%, far below Q2's roughly 60% surge. Samsung shares slipped as investors questioned the durability of the memory rally; detailed results are due October 29.
