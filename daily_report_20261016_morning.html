US September CPI rises 3.7% year-on-year, above expectations; Fed October hike odds at 37%
美国9月CPI同比上涨3.7%，高于预期；美联储10月加息概率约37%
US consumer prices rose 3.7% in September from a year earlier, above economists' forecasts of 3.6%. Core CPI rose 2.6% year-on-year. Gasoline prices surged 9.1% month-on-month. Following the data, markets priced in about a 37% chance of a Fed rate hike at the October 27-28 meeting, up from 22% previously. Most economists still expect the Fed to hold in October and hike in December.
美国9月消费者价格指数同比上涨3.7%，高于经济学家预期的3.6%。核心CPI同比上涨2.6%。汽油价格环比飙升9.1%。数据公布后，市场预期美联储在10月27-28日会议上加息的概率约为37%，此前为22%。多数经济学家仍预计美联储10月按兵不动，12月加息。
reuters.com⭐⭐⭐
Fed's Daly: No urgency to hike in October, but December move likely
美联储戴利：10月加息不紧迫，但12月行动可能性大
San Francisco Fed President Mary Daly said the Fed will probably need to raise rates further to rein in inflation, but signaled no urgency to act at the October meeting. 'We are more likely than not to need additional adjustments, but the timing is something we're still deliberating,' Daly told Reuters. Several colleagues have also signaled the Fed is unlikely to raise rates this month.
旧金山联储主席戴利表示，美联储可能需要进一步加息以控制通胀，但暗示10月会议没有紧迫性。戴利告诉路透社：'我们很可能需要进一步调整，但时机仍在商议中。'几位同事也暗示美联储本月不太可能加息。

Oil prices fall as US considers easing sanctions on shadow fleet vessels
美国考虑放宽对影子船队制裁，油价下跌
Oil prices fell more than $1 in early trade on Thursday as investors weighed supply risks from Hormuz attacks against US efforts to cut shipping costs. The Trump administration is considering easing sanctions on shadow fleet vessels that transport oil from Russia, Iran and Venezuela. Brent crude fell to $104.67 while WTI dropped to $94.07.
周四早盘油价下跌逾1美元，投资者权衡霍尔木兹海峡袭击带来的供应风险与美国降低航运成本的努力。特朗普政府正在考虑放宽对从俄罗斯、伊朗和委内瑞拉运输石油的影子船队船只的制裁。布伦特原油跌至104.67美元，WTI跌至94.07美元。

Iran deploys new tactics in Strait of Hormuz to keep oil prices high ahead of US midterms
伊朗在霍尔木兹海峡部署新战术以在美国中期选举前维持高油价
Two senior Iranian officials told Reuters that Tehran is deploying new tactics to target ships sailing through the Strait of Hormuz, seeking to keep oil prices high and influence the US midterm elections. The strategy involves changes in both the type and pace of attacks. A container ship and dry bulk carrier sank near the strait in the past 24 hours after being hit by projectiles.
两名伊朗高级官员告诉路透社，德黑兰正在部署新战术以打击通过霍尔木兹海峡的船只，试图维持高油价并影响美国中期选举。该策略涉及改变攻击的类型和节奏。过去24小时内，一艘集装箱船和一艘干散货船在海峡附近被弹丸击中后沉没。

ASML posts record Q3 on AI chip demand, warns supply won't meet demand through 2028
ASML第三季度业绩创纪录受AI芯片需求推动，警告供应至2028年仍无法满足需求
ASML reported record Q3 net sales of EUR11.7 billion and net profit of EUR4.96 billion. The company raised its full-year sales outlook to EUR44-45 billion. CEO Christophe Fouquet said AI-driven demand is so strong that even planned 30% capacity increases in 2027 and 2028 won't fully meet customer needs. Some clients are already inquiring about orders for 2029 and 2030.
ASML第三季度净销售额创纪录达117亿欧元，净利润49.6亿欧元。公司上调全年销售预期至440-450亿欧元。首席执行官Christophe Fouquet表示，AI驱动的需求如此强劲，即使计划在2027年和2028年将产能提高30%，也无法完全满足客户需求。部分客户已开始询问2029年和2030年的订单。
