SpaceX heads for larger Nasdaq 100 weighting as share unlocks lift float
SpaceX 纳指权重将上调，解禁推升自由流通股比例
SpaceX shares rose 3.73% to $153.47, their highest close since July 7, lifting market value back above $2 trillion on expectations that its Nasdaq 100 weighting will rise at the index's quarterly rebalance. Nasdaq caps a constituent's weight at the lower of its total shares outstanding or three times its free float, which left SpaceX at just 1.25% and 19th despite being the index's sixth-largest company by market value. More than 1 billion shares have since unlocked, lifting free float from under 10% shortly after the June listing to close to 30%. Strategists at JPMorgan estimated the weighting could rise to between 1.51% and 2.25%, forcing index funds and exchange-tracked funds to buy between $12.4 billion and $15.5 billion of the stock. The change is announced after Friday's close and takes effect September 21.
SpaceX股价上涨3.73%至153.47美元，创7月7日以来最高收盘价，市值重回2万亿美元上方，市场预期其纳斯达克100指数权重将在季度再平衡中上调。纳斯达克在计算成分股权重时，取「总流通股数」与「自由流通股数的三倍」两者中较低值，这使SpaceX尽管市值排名指数第六，权重却仅为1.25%、位列第19。此后已有超过10亿股解禁，自由流通股比例由6月上市后不久的不足10%升至接近30%。摩根大通策略师估计权重可能升至1.51%至2.25%，迫使指数基金与ETF被动买入124亿至155亿美元的该股。调整将于本周五收盘后公布，9月21日生效。

日元企稳于2月来最强附近，日央行加息押注升温
The yen held gains around 153.4 per dollar after rallying nearly 4% over the past week, its largest weekly advance since July 2024, as traders priced a near-certain Bank of Japan rate increase this month and a roughly one-in-four chance of a further move as soon as October.
