SpaceX Starship Flight 14 set for first orbital launch on September 28
SpaceX星舰第14飞定于9月28日首次入轨发射
SpaceX received FAA regulatory approval for Starship Flight 14, targeting Monday September 28 at 8:15 a.m. EDT from Starbase, Texas. The mission will be Starship's first orbital flight, deploying 26 Starlink V3 satellites. The Super Heavy booster will return to the Gulf of Mexico while the upper stage completes six orbits before splashing down in the Pacific.
SpaceX获得FAA发射许可，星舰第14飞定于9月28日美国东部时间上午8:15从德州星基地发射。这将是星舰首次入轨飞行，部署26颗Starlink V3卫星。超重型助推器将返回墨西哥湾，上级飞行器完成六圈轨道飞行后在太平洋溅落。

Oil prices fall as Iran proposes reopening Hormuz within seven days
伊朗提议七日内重开霍尔木兹海峡，油价下跌
WTI crude fell 2.32% to $92.41 per barrel and Brent dropped 2.14% to $104.32 on Friday after Iran proposed a seven-day plan to reopen the Strait of Hormuz. The proposal, conveyed through Qatari mediators, offered a potential diplomatic off-ramp that eased supply concerns and reduced the geopolitical premium in oil prices.
周五WTI原油下跌2.32%至92.41美元/桶，布伦特下跌2.14%至104.32美元/桶。此前伊朗通过卡塔尔调解方提出七日内重开霍尔木兹海峡的计划，这一外交突破缓解了供应担忧并降低了油价中的地缘溢价。

US and Iran explore phased deal to reopen Hormuz Strait, sources say
美伊探讨阶段性协议重开霍尔木兹海峡
US and Iranian negotiators in New York are exploring a phased path out of war that would involve Tehran reopening the Strait of Hormuz and Washington lifting its economic blockade of Iran. Federal Reserve signals further tightening as inflation remains elevated
美联储暗示将进一步收紧政策，通胀仍处高位
The Federal Reserve raised rates by 25bp to 3.75%-4.00% on September 16, its first hike since July 2023. Chair Kevin Warsh said inflation remains too high and the Committee's unanimous vote shows resolve to achieve price stability. The Fed projected the policy rate would reach 4.00%-4.25% by year-end, with 12 of 18 officials expecting another hike.
美联储9月16日加息25个基点至3.75%-4.00%，为2023年7月以来首次加息。主席沃什表示通胀仍然过高，委员会一致投票显示实现价格稳定的决心。联储预计年底政策利率将达4.00%-4.25%，18位官员中有12位预期再加息一次。
