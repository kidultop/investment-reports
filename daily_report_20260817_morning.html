Trump instructs Pentagon to reduce military exercises with South Korea
特朗普指示五角大楼缩减美韩联合军演规模
U.S. President Donald Trump said on Sunday he had instructed the Pentagon to substantially reduce joint military exercises with South Korea in a social media post that also mentioned Seoul's refusal to take part in actions against Iran. Trump said on Truth Social that, although it was too late to cancel the exercises entirely, he was 'not happy' that the United States had previously agreed to participate. He cited his 'very good relationship' with North Korean leader Kim Jong Un and said the exercises were costly and sent a signal that was 'totally inappropriate and hostile.' The annual Ulchi Freedom Shield joint military drills are scheduled to run from August 17 to 27. South Korea's defence ministry said the exercises would proceed as scheduled.
美国总统特朗普周日表示，已指示五角大楼大幅缩减与韩国的联合军演规模，并在社交媒体帖子中提到首尔拒绝参与针对伊朗的行动。特朗普在Truth Social上称，尽管现在完全取消演习为时已晚，但他对美国此前同意参演“感到不满”。他提到与朝鲜领导人金正恩“关系非常好”，并称军演耗资巨大，向朝鲜发出“完全不恰当且敌对”的信号。年度“乙支自由护盾”联合军演定于8月17日至27日举行。韩国国防部表示演习将按计划进行。

油价上涨，美国数据削弱美联储加息预期

彭博：日本长期收益率上升或可支撑日元

Fed's Goolsbee says latest inflation data is better
芝加哥联储主席古尔斯比：最新通胀数据有所好转
Chicago Federal Reserve Bank President Austan Goolsbee said on Thursday that the latest U.S. inflation data has been a 'little better,' and he is hopeful that as the effects of tariffs and higher oil prices from the Iran war fade, inflation can continue to improve. 'If we can get some of this stuff into the rearview mirror then I think we get back on what I was calling the golden path, which is inflation heading back to 2%,' Goolsbee said. The Fed last month held the short-term policy rate in the 3.50%-3.75% range, though at least five of the central bank's 19 policymakers wanted to raise rates instead. As recently as last week traders had been pricing in a Fed rate hike as soon as September, but back-to-back reports of milder-than-expected CPI and PPI in July, following the weak jobs report, have mostly erased expectations of any near-term Fed move.
芝加哥联储主席古尔斯比周四表示，美国最新通胀数据“略有好转”，他期待随着伊朗战争导致的关税和油价上涨影响消退，通胀能够继续改善。古尔斯比称：“如果这些因素能成为过去，我认为我们将回到我所称的‘黄金路径’，即通胀重回2%。”美联储上月将短期政策利率维持在3.50%-3.75%区间，但19位决策者中至少5位希望加息。就在上周，交易员还押注美联储最早9月加息，但7月CPI和PPI连续低于预期、叠加疲弱就业报告后，市场对美联储短期内采取行动的押注已基本消退。
