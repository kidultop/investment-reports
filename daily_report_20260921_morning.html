Oil prices rise as Houthis strike Riyadh; Brent climbs 0.78% toward $105
胡塞武装袭击利雅得，周一油价反弹：布伦特涨0.78%逼近105美元
Oil prices rose on Monday after Yemen's Iran-backed Houthis launched missile and drone strikes at Saudi Arabia's capital Riyadh, escalating Middle East tension while the US and Iran remain in a stalemate. Brent crude futures climbed 81 cents, or 0.78%, to $104.68 a barrel, while US West Texas Intermediate crude gained 0.76% to $101.06, extending a week in which crude benchmarks stayed near $100 amid Hormuz shipping disruptions.

Iran says Strait of Hormuz stays closed until US commitments met as UN General Assembly week begins
伊朗称霍尔木兹海峡将维持关闭直至美方兑现承诺，联合国大会周开启
Iran said the Strait of Hormuz will remain closed until its conditions are met and US commitments implemented, prolonging uncertainty for global oil and crude shipping. Preliminary Kpler data reported by Reuters showed only four commodity vessels transited Hormuz on September 17 versus a 10-day average of 16, while Brent settled the week near $104. President Trump signaled openness to talks around the UN General Assembly, where he meets six Gulf leaders on September 22, and warned Tehran of economic collapse or military action if no deal is reached; the US Strategic Petroleum Reserve has fallen to its lowest since 1982.
伊朗表示，在其条件得到满足、美国承诺得到落实之前，霍尔木兹海峡将维持关闭，全球石油与原油航运的不确定性延长。路透援引Kpler初步数据显示，9月17日仅有4艘商船通过霍尔木兹海峡，远低于10日均值16艘；布伦特原油上周收于104美元附近。特朗普释放联大周谈判意愿，并将于9月22日与海湾六国领导人会晤，同时警告伊朗若无法达成协议将面临经济崩溃或军事打击；美国战略石油储备已降至1982年以来最低。

Bank of England holds rates at 3.75% but warns more tightening may be needed if Iran war drags on
英国央行维持利率3.75%不变，但警告若伊朗战争拖延或需进一步紧缩
The Bank of England left rates unchanged at 3.75% for a sixth straight meeting but warned that further tightening may be needed if the war in Iran drags on and keeps oil and crude prices elevated, adding to the sense that policymakers are getting ahead of an energy-driven inflation shock across global bond and treasury markets.
英国央行连续第六次会议维持3.75%利率不变，但警告若伊朗战争持续推高油价与原油价格，可能需要进一步紧缩。市场普遍认为，全球央行正在抢先应对能源驱动的通胀冲击，全球债市与美债市场随之波动。

European shares tumble as autos, telecoms lead sell-off; Volkswagen plunges on profit warning
欧洲股市大跌：汽车与电信领跌，大众汽车盈利预警重挫股价
Europe's STOXX 600 fell 1.1% on Friday in broad losses led by automobile and telecom shares, with the FTSE 100 down 1.45% and Germany's DAX down 1.60%. Volkswagen led the declines, down 5.6% after slashing its outlook and flagging about 10 billion euros in one-off items tied to Porsche, job-cut provisions and a weak Chinese market. 受汽车与电信股领跌影响，泛欧STOXX 600指数周五大跌1.1%，英国富时100跌1.45%、德国DAX跌1.60%。大众汽车下调全年展望并披露与保时捷相关的约100亿欧元一次性减值及裁员拨备，股价重挫5.6%领跌；雀巢因俄罗斯查封其在俄资产下跌2.6%，食品饮料板块同样承受通胀压力。

Fed raises rates 25bp to 3.75%-4.00%, first hike since 2023, dot plot signals more
美联储加息25个基点至3.75%-4.00%，2023年以来首次加息，点阵图暗示还会加息
The FOMC voted 12-0 to raise the federal funds rate by a quarter point to 3.75-4.00 percent, the first hike since 2023, as inflation remains elevated and the Committee pledged to deliver price stability. Bank of Japan hikes to 1.25%, highest since 1995, as Ueda vows more increases; yen slides to 157
日本央行加息至1.25%创31年新高，植田暗示继续加息；日元仍贬至157
The Bank of Japan raised its policy interest rate by 25bp to around 1.25%, the highest since 1995, in a 7-2 vote, saying it will continue to raise the policy rate as underlying CPI inflation approaches 2% amid higher oil import prices and a weak yen. Governor Ueda said the bank will keep raising rates while watching the impact, but two dissents signalled a slower pace; the yen still weakened to the 157 per dollar level after the central bank interest rate decision.
日本央行以7票对2票将政策利率上调25个基点至约1.25%，创1995年以来最高水平，并表示在潜在CPI通胀逼近2%、油价推升进口成本及日元疲弱的背景下将继续加息。行长植田和男称将在关注影响的同时继续推进加息，但两张反对票暗示后续节奏或放缓；利率决议公布后日元兑美元仍一度跌至157。
