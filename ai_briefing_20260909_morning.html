arXiv Bayesian 统一框架:SFT / ICL / RLHF/RLVR 视为 forward-KL 投影
arXiv 2609.05111 — Bayesian unification of SFT/ICL/RLHF/RLVR as forward-KL projections
Research · 理论
Junxin Fan (arXiv 2609.05111) 提出 Bayesian 统一视角:监督微调 (SFT)、上下文学习 (ICL)、KL 正则化 RLHF/RLVR 都是前向 KL 投影到广义 Bayes 后验,差异只在学习信号来源与应用方式。该框架解释了"为什么 few-shot prompting 在 RL 调优模型上效果参差"以及"推理流水线为何需要监督预热"。
Junxin Fan (arXiv 2609.05111) introduces a Bayesian view: supervised fine-tuning (SFT), in-context learning (ICL), and KL-regularized RLHF/RLVR are all forward-KL projections onto a generalized Bayes posterior — their differences lie purely in how the learning signal is sourced. The framework explains why few-shot prompting behaves erratically on RL-tuned models and why reasoning pipelines require supervised warm-up.
为何重要：首批将 RL 后训练阶段所有主流范式置于同一理论的统一框架,为研究人员设计"配方组合"提供理论指引,有助 DeepSeek R1/o1 类管线的可重复性。
Why it matters：The first framework that places all mainstream post-training paradigms under one theory, with direct implications for reproducibility of DeepSeek-R1/o1 style reasoning pipelines.

Chang Xia et al. (arXiv 2609.05069) present the Debate-Mixture-of-Agents (DMoA) framework — structured, role-based debate across multiple LLM agents with iterative reasoning and an auditable trace. Across 297 rare-disease and 1,719 complex clinical cases, DMoA improves top-1 diagnostic accuracy by 10.21pp and safety rate by 11.36pp over a GPT-4o baseline; ablations confirm gains come from the workflow structure, not from more models.
为何重要：在高风险场景中,"结构胜于模型"——可审计的多智能体辩论在医疗诊断上首次有 10pp 级别的硬证据,直接影响医院 AI 部署的审批框架。
Why it matters：First hard evidence in high-stakes settings that "structure beats scale" — auditable multi-agent debate adds 10pp in medical diagnosis, shifting hospital AI deployment governance frameworks.
