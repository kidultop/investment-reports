US forces strike two Iranian launchers on Iran's Larak island, US official says
美军空袭伊朗拉拉克岛两处发射装置，为7月底以来首次
U.S. forces struck two Iranian launchers on Iran's Larak island on Sunday, a U.S. official said, marking the first known American strikes on Iran since late July. The official said Islamic Revolutionary Guard Corps forces were observed preparing to launch rockets with sea mines into the Strait of Hormuz. Iran's Revolutionary Guards said the attack killed and wounded several soldiers and citizens and would be met with a response and punishment from Tehran; Iran later fired ballistic missiles at U.S. forces stationed in Jordan. The war, which began on February 28, has now crossed its six-month mark and 18 U.S. service members have been killed.
一名美国官员表示，美军周日打击了伊朗拉拉克岛上的两处发射装置，这是自7月下旬以来已知的美军首次对伊朗发动打击。该官员称，当时观察到伊斯兰革命卫队正准备向霍尔木兹海峡发射携带水雷的火箭弹。伊朗革命卫队表示袭击造成多名士兵与平民死伤，德黑兰将予以「回应和惩罚」；伊朗随后向驻约旦美军发射弹道导弹。这场始于2月28日的战争已超过六个月，已有18名美军人员身亡。

Wall Street ends lower after Fed Chair Warsh reaffirms inflation fight
沃什重申抗通胀决心，华尔街三大指数周五集体收跌
Wall Street's main indexes ended lower on Friday as investors turned cautious after Federal Reserve Chair Kevin Warsh reiterated the central bank's focus on fighting inflation, increasing prospects for a rate hike. The S&P 500 lost 19.23 points, or 0.25%, to 7,711.76; the Nasdaq Composite fell 138.93 points, or 0.52%, to 26,402.42; and the Dow slipped 9.45 points, or 0.02%, to 53,559.99. Nvidia slipped 4.6% and Marvell Technology tumbled 10.3% on doubts over the timing of revenue from its AI chip agreement with Alphabet's Google, while Alphabet gained 1.7% and Apple rose 1.6%. For the week the S&P 500 still gained 0.49%, the Nasdaq 0.85% and the Dow 0.53%.
周五华尔街主要指数集体收跌，投资者在美联储主席沃什重申以抗通胀为政策重心后转向谨慎，加息前景升温。标普500指数跌19.23点或0.25%至7711.76点，纳斯达克综合指数跌138.93点或0.52%至26402.42点，道指微跌9.45点或0.02%至53559.99点。英伟达跌4.6%，迈威尔科技因市场质疑其与谷歌AI芯片协议收入确认时点而重挫10.3%；Alphabet涨1.7%，苹果涨1.6%。全周标普500仍累涨0.49%，纳指涨0.85%，道指涨0.53%。

VIEW Rate-hike expectations rise on Warsh speech at Jackson Hole
观点：沃什杰克逊霍尔讲话推升加息预期
The Federal Reserve will have work to do if policymakers are not confident that underlying inflation is returning to its 2% target, Chair Kevin Warsh said on Friday in remarks that marked the closest he has come to acknowledging interest rate hikes may be needed to ease price pressures. After the comments, bets that rates would be raised at the September meeting rose to as much as a 55.7% probability from 35.4% on Thursday, according to CME Group's FedWatch tool. Short-dated Treasury yields climbed with the dollar, with the 2-year yield hitting its highest in a month and the dollar index up as much as 0.6% for its biggest daily climb in two and a half months.
美联储主席沃什周五表示，若决策者无法确信基础通胀正回归2%目标，美联储「还有工作要做」——这是他迄今最接近承认可能需要加息以缓解物价压力的表态。据CME FedWatch工具，9月加息概率一度从周四的35.4%升至55.7%。短端美债收益率随美元同步走高，两年期收益率触及一个月高位，美元指数一度上涨0.6%，创两个半月来最大单日涨幅。

Marvell Leads AI Stocks Lower After Earnings That Narrowly Topped Estimates
迈威尔财报仅微幅超预期，领跌AI板块
Marvell Technology shares dropped Friday after the company narrowly beat earnings and revenue estimates, leading several AI-related stocks lower. Marvell posted revenue of $2.74 billion and adjusted earnings of 94 cents per share, each narrowly topping the Visible Alpha consensus of $2.71 billion and 93 cents. The midpoint of its third-quarter revenue and adjusted EPS forecasts also slightly beat estimates at $3.15 billion and $1.10. Jefferies analysts called it somewhat disappointing that there was not more upside from the custom chip deal with Google in Marvell's projections. Shares of Nvidia, Intel, AMD, Micron and Sandisk also slipped, and Marvell remains up over 160% in 2026.
迈威尔科技周五股价下挫，因其财报与营收仅微幅超出预期，并拖累多只AI相关个股走低。公司季度营收27.4亿美元、调整后每股收益94美分，略高于Visible Alpha一致预期的27.1亿美元和93美分；三季度指引中值31.5亿美元、每股收益1.10美元同样小幅超预期。Jefferies分析师称，迈威尔的预测中未能体现更多来自谷歌定制芯片协议的上行空间，令人有些失望。英伟达、英特尔、AMD、美光与闪迪同步走低；迈威尔年内涨幅仍超160%。
