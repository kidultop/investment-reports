Anthropic 成立生命科学研究组与湿实验室：Claude 自主发现新型 ART 酶系统2026-09-23
Claude Autonomously Discovers a Novel ART Enzyme System in Anthropic's New Wet Lab
Anthropic 9 月 23 日宣布组建生命科学研究组并在湾区建立湿实验室，专注用 Claude 做基础生物学研究。首个成果：约 950 个 Claude Agent 花 21 小时、消耗约 2.1 亿 token 扫描海量 DNA 序列库，自主发现一种与 CRISPR 相似的 DNA 重复阵列相关的新型酶系统——阵列相关逆转录酶（ART）。人类参与仅限初始提示与实验室验证；该系统集可编程操作系统（切割、复制、粘贴 DNA）的若干特征于一身，功能尚待确认，预印本已发布。CRISPR 先驱、MIT/Broad 研究所张锋评价这是"AI Agent 参与生物学发现的激动人心的案例"。
Anthropic unveiled a life sciences research group and a Bay Area wet lab on Sept 23. In an early result, roughly 950 Claude agents spent 21 hours and about 210 million tokens scanning DNA databases, autonomously spotting a novel enzyme system — array-associated reverse transcriptases (ART) — with CRISPR-like DNA repeat arrays. Human involvement was limited to the initial prompt and lab validation; a preprint is out, and CRISPR pioneer Feng Zhang called it "an exciting example of how AI agents can contribute to biological discovery."
为何重要 Why it matters：若 ART 功能得到确认，它将成为继 CRISPR 之后又一个可编程 DNA 操作系统的候选；更关键的是展示了「Agent 大规模筛选数据 + 人类只做实验验证」的科研新范式，把 AI 从文献助手推向自主发现的第一线。
If ART's function is confirmed, it could become a candidate programmable DNA-operation system beyond CRISPR; more broadly, it previews a discovery pipeline where agents mine data at scale and humans only run the experiments — AI moving from assistant to autonomous discoverer.

Google DeepMind：Private AI Compute 引入持久化安全记忆，密钥只留在用户设备2026-09-23
DeepMind Adds Persistent, Encrypted Server-Side Memory to Private AI Compute
Google DeepMind 9 月 23 日发布 Private AI Compute 架构技术更新：为云端 AI 助手增加持久化记忆层，用户上下文封存在专用加密存储中，解密密钥仅保存在个人设备上——官方称数据"连 Google 自己也无法读取"。模型需要访问记忆时，设备通过端到端加密通道连接云端安全飞地（secure enclave），在隔离内存中临时解密、保存新上下文后立即重新加密。此前该平台严格"无状态"（任务结束即清空上下文）。Google 同步发布防篡改的服务器软件公开记录、更新版技术简报与第三方安全审计结果，并邀请外部隐私研究者验证。
On Sept 23 Google DeepMind detailed a persistent memory layer for Private AI Compute: user context is sealed in encrypted cloud storage whose cryptographic keys live only on personal devices — inaccessible, Google says, even to Google. Requests run inside hardware secure enclaves that temporarily decrypt, update and re-encrypt context, replacing the platform's earlier strictly stateless design. Google is publishing a tamper-proof public record of its server software, an updated technical brief and an independent audit, inviting outside researchers to verify.
为何重要 Why it matters：记忆是个性化 AI 的核心，也是隐私争议的焦点；「云端算力 + 设备端密钥」若经外部研究者验证成立，可能成为跨设备 AI 助手的隐私新基线，直接影响 Gemini 与 Android 生态的后续产品形态。
Memory is the core of personal AI and its biggest privacy flashpoint; if external researchers verify the enclave design, device-held keys could set a new privacy baseline for cross-device assistants across Gemini and Android.

arXiv 新研究：聊天模板像开关一样控制 LLM 的自述语气，激活导向可复现该行为2026-09-23
Chat Templates Act as a Switch on LLM Self-Reports, and Activation Steering Reproduces It
9 月 23 日 arXiv 新论文（已被 COLM 2026 Workshop 收录）发现：聊天模板显著影响模型谈论自身的方式——有模板时，"我只是个 AI"式免责声明语气上升、"我感觉"式体验语气下降；去掉模板则反转。研究在 8 个 9B 参数级以下开源指令模型上验证，并在 3 个模型的激活空间中定位到控制该行为的方向：移除该方向可降低免责语气，添加则增强，且在无模板模型中加入该方向即可复现免责行为。作者提醒：研究模型自述或"内省"的结论可能被部署模板混淆。
A Sept 23 arXiv paper (COLM 2026 workshop) shows chat templates act like a switch on self-referential voice: with a template, disclaimer-style "I'm just an AI" rises and experiential "I feel" falls; without one, the pattern reverses across 8 open instruct models (≤9B). A direction in the activation space of 3 models steers the behavior — adding it to template-free models reproduces the disclaiming voice. The authors warn that studies of model introspection are confounded by deployment templates.
为何重要 Why it matters：直接关联当下关于模型自我报告、内省与安全的辩论——若自述语气由部署模板驱动，把它当作对齐证据的研究需要重新审视；也再次提醒「模型说的」与「模型是什么」之间存在可测量的距离。
It bears directly on the current debate over model self-reports and introspection: if the voice is template-driven, evidence built on it needs re-examination — a measurable gap between what a model says and what it is.

arXiv：联邦学习接入量子-经典混合模型，隐私约束下准确率接近集中式训练2026-09-23
Federated Quantum-Classical Learning Keeps Data Local While Nearing Centralized Accuracy
9 月 23 日 arXiv 论文将联邦学习引入量子机器学习（QML）：在垂直联邦场景下，使用 SBVFL（Split Blind Vertical Federated Learning）协议连接量子-经典混合主动方与经典被动方，避免原始数据集中。在自建 SMPP 基准上，混合模型准确率从本地训练的 0.723 升至 0.876，接近非隐私的集中式训练水平，且可训练参数显著少于经典神经网络与随机森林对照。作者称该结果为隐私敏感多方场景下的量子-经典协作提供了可行路径。
A Sept 23 arXiv paper brings federated learning to quantum machine learning: a Split Blind Vertical FL protocol links a hybrid quantum-classical active party with a classical passive party without centralizing raw data. On the SMPP benchmark, accuracy rises from 0.723 (local training) to 0.876 — close to non-private centralized training — with far fewer trainable parameters than classical baselines.
为何重要 Why it matters：量子机器学习长期受制于「数据不能出门」的隐私约束；若联邦化路径在真实多方场景可扩展，将为金融、医疗等强合规机构提供探索 QML 的入口。
Privacy has long boxed in QML; if the federated route scales to real multi-party settings, it gives finance and healthcare — where data cannot leave the building — a compliant on-ramp to quantum-classical learning.

On Sept 23 Gov. Newsom named four experts — Goldman, Hadfield, Nelson and Reich — to advise on his AI executive order (N-9-26), weighing rules that would embed independent verifiers in frontier labs and require emergency "kill switches" for frontier models; the panel first meets in November. California is the first US state to move from disclosure rules toward model-level emergency-intervention design; the kill switch is technically contested (distributed deployments defy a physical off-switch), but the independent-verification framework may become the de facto standard first — frontier labs are directly in scope.

Hugging Face 发布 ReLoRe：把「仓库记忆」做成编码 Agent 的基础设施2026-09-21
Hugging Face's ReLoRe Turns Repository Memory into Infrastructure for Coding Agents
Hugging Face 9 月 21 日发布 ReLoRe 项目，将编码 Agent 的记忆从一次性聊天记录升级为可持续维护的「仓库记忆」基础设施：以 Lance 生态为存储底座，把决策、约束、失败尝试等作为记忆单元，每条记录指向具体文件、commit、测试与负责人，支持跨会话检索、删除与修正。项目同时给出评估框架：统计重复提问、错误召回、不必要编辑与纠错后的恢复速度。博客强调这是开源方向的开端，而非"记忆问题已解决"的宣告。
Hugging Face's ReLoRe project (Sept 21) upgrades coding-agent memory from disposable chat transcripts to durable repository memory: built on the Lance ecosystem, it stores decisions, constraints and failed approaches as records linking back to files, commits, tests and owners, with cross-session retrieval, deletion and correction controls plus an evaluation rubric measuring repeated questions, bad recalls and recovery after corrections. The post frames it as an open-source direction, not a solved problem.
为何重要 Why it matters：编码 Agent 的下一个瓶颈不是模型而是上下文管理；「记忆可审计、可删除、可修正」直指当前 Agent 记忆系统的通病——过时的架构建议是延迟触发的缺陷，而记忆条目若无人审阅会悄悄变成"政策"。
The next bottleneck for coding agents is context, not models; making memory auditable, deletable and correctable targets the biggest failure mode — stale architecture advice that fires months later, and generated memories quietly becoming policy when no one reviews them.
