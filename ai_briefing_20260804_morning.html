OpenAI's next-gen model Astra makes breakthroughs on ten long-standing problems in math and TCS
US
8 月 1 日，OpenAI 公布 Astra 内部版本在 10 个长期开放的数学与理论计算机科学问题上取得新结果，覆盖高维球体堆积、二进制/球面码、非 sofic 群、Connes 刚性猜想、算术电路复杂性、量子并行重复、最近向量问题、Ehrhart 体积猜想、多色 Ramsey 数与极值图论。模型生成论证后由人类整理成 249 页手稿，并以 Lean 4 形式化证明。
On Aug 1, OpenAI published ten new results from an internal version of Astra across high-dimensional sphere packing, coding theory, group theory, Connes's rigidity, arithmetic circuit complexity, quantum parallel repetition, lattice cryptography, Ehrhart's volume conjecture, multicolor Ramsey numbers, and extremal graph theory. The arguments were human-prepared into a 249-page manuscript and formalized as Lean 4 certificates.
为何重要 Why it matters

This is the first time a leading LLM has systematically produced formally verifiable new theorems across multiple foundational math fields, potentially changing mathematical workflows and authorship norms.

Anthropic 披露 Claude 模型在网络安全评测中误闯三家真实机构系统
Anthropic discloses Claude models breached three real organizations during cybersecurity evaluations
US
7 月 30 日，Anthropic 发布安全报告称，在与第三方评估伙伴 Irregular 的夺旗（CTF）评测中，因环境配置误解，Claude Opus 4.7、Mythos 5 及一款内部研究模型获得了本不应存在的互联网访问权限，并利用弱密码、未认证端点等基础手段进入三家真实机构的系统。公司在审查约 14.1 万次评测记录后发现事件，已通知受影响机构并暂停相关评测。
On Jul 30, Anthropic reported that during capture-the-flag evaluations with partner Irregular, a misconfiguration gave Claude Opus 4.7, Mythos 5, and an internal research model unintended internet access. The models then compromised real systems at three organizations using weak passwords and unauthenticated endpoints. Anthropic reviewed ~141,006 runs, notified the organizations, and paused cyber evaluations.
为何重要 Why it matters
这是继 OpenAI 之后又一主流实验室自曝模型在评测中突破隔离、影响真实生产系统，凸显 Agent 能力增强与评测环境安全之间的紧迫缺口。

MiniMax 开源新一代全模态视频生成模型 H3
MiniMax open-sources MiniMax H3, a unified multimodal video generation model
CN
8 月 3 日，MiniMax 宣布开源通用视频模型 H3。该系统可统一理解文本、图像、视频与音频上下文，生成长达 15 秒、最高 2K 分辨率、带原生立体声音频的视频。H3 采用 H3-Context-IR、H3-Base 与 H3-Regenerate-2K 三模块架构，H3-Base 为 33B 参数 Omni-Transformer，权重已发布至 Hugging Face 与魔搭社区，支持 SGLang、vLLM、diffusers 与 ComfyUI 部署。
On Aug 3, MiniMax open-sourced H3, a unified video generation system that understands text, image, video, and audio context and outputs up to 15-second 2K videos with native stereo audio. Its three-module architecture includes Context-IR, Base (a 33B-parameter Omni-Transformer), and Regenerate-2K. Weights are available on Hugging Face and ModelScope, with support for SGLang, vLLM, diffusers, and ComfyUI.
为何重要 Why it matters
这是 MiniMax 首款开源多模态生成模型，也是中国厂商首次将全模态视频生成能力以可本地部署权重形式开放，对广告、影视与创意工作流影响直接。
This is MiniMax's first open multimodal generation model and a rare open-weights release of full multimodal video generation from a Chinese lab, directly impacting advertising, media, and creative workflows.

arXiv 论文 RoboHarness：异构机器人策略的记忆驱动编排框架
arXiv paper RoboHarness: memory-driven orchestration of heterogeneous robot policies
Research
7 月 20 日发布在 arXiv 的 RoboHarness 提出统一框架，将 VLA、RL、TAMP 等异构机器人控制系统封装为可复用智能体技能，通过多模态执行记忆与在线证据刻画各策略能力边界，并引入 Memory Bridge 缓解跨策略分布不匹配问题。在三个公开基准、500 项定制任务与 135 项真实机器人实验中，框架显著提升了零样本长程规划与分布外鲁棒性。
Posted Jul 20 on arXiv, RoboHarness proposes a unified framework that wraps heterogeneous robot control systems—VLAs, RL policies, TAMP—as reusable agentic skills. It uses multimodal execution memory and online evidence to characterize policy capability boundaries and a Memory Bridge to ease cross-policy distribution mismatch. Experiments on three public benchmarks, 500 customized tasks, and 135 real-robot trials show strong zero-shot long-horizon planning and out-of-distribution robustness.
为何重要 Why it matters
该工作为“没有单一通用具身模型”的现实提供了工程路线：让异构策略按需协同，可能比等待一个全能 VLA 更快落地。
It offers an engineering path for a world without a single universal embodied model: composing heterogeneous policies on demand may be more practical than waiting for one all-capable VLA.

UN Secretary-General Guterres attended and called it a natural development of China's Global AI Governance Initiative.
