阿里 Qwen-UI-Agent 正式发布：真机驱动、跨端 GUI 智能体基座，多项基准刷新 SOTA
Alibaba launches Qwen-UI-Agent: a real-device-driven cross-platform GUI agent foundation model with new SOTA benchmarks

8 月 20 日，阿里千问大模型正式推出 Qwen-UI-Agent，定位为面向真实设备的 GUI 智能体基座模型，覆盖移动端、PC 桌面端、网页及深度搜索环境。模型直接通过"看懂屏幕 + 模拟人类操作"完成 App 与软件任务，覆盖大量无 API 的传统应用。

官方公布的基准表现：移动端 MobileWorld 82.1%、MobileWorld-Real 92.2%、AndroidDaily 97.5%；PC 端 OSWorld-Verified 79.5%；浏览器 WebArena 73.6%；ScreenSpot-Pro 81.5%。综合表现对标或超越 GPT-5.6 Sol、Claude Opus 4.8、Gemini 3.1 Pro、Seed 2.1 Pro 等海外旗舰。模型基于覆盖 100+ 台真机、150+ 应用的训练集群，并在超 100 步长轨迹上做在线 RL，约 10000 个并发环境同时 rollout。

安全设计贯穿全流程：高危请求直接拒绝；支付、删除、授权类操作须用户确认才继续；支持 GUI + CLI 混合动作空间，且具备跨设备主动服务能力（航班取消自动改签、手机电脑接力）。

On August 20, Alibaba's Qwen team officially released Qwen-UI-Agent, a GUI agent foundation model for real-world devices spanning mobile, desktop PC, web, and DeepSearch. It operates by "reading screens and mimicking human actions," opening a path to automating legacy software with no public APIs.

Reported benchmarks: MobileWorld 82.1%, MobileWorld-Real 92.2%, AndroidDaily 97.5% (mobile); OSWorld-Verified 79.5% (PC); WebArena 73.6% (browser); ScreenSpot-Pro 81.5% (grounding). It matches or surpasses GPT-5.6 Sol, Claude Opus 4.8, Gemini 3.1 Pro, and Seed 2.1 Pro. Training uses 100+ real phones and 150+ apps, with 10K concurrent environments doing online RL on 100+ step trajectories.

Safety runs end-to-end: high-risk requests are refused outright, while payment, deletion, and authorization actions require explicit user confirmation. The model supports a GUI+CLI hybrid action space and offers proactive cross-device service (e.g., automatically rebooking after flight cancellation).

Why it matters / 为何重要
Qwen-UI-Agent 把"真机训练 + 真机评测"作为 GUI Agent 的新基线，并首次系统性把"安全护栏内置于 Agent 执行链路"。它意味着 OpenAI Operator/Claude Computer Use 之外的第三条真机 Agent 路线已经成熟，且中国实验室具备完整工业化能力——这将加速办公、客服、本地生活等场景的 Agent 化进程。
Qwen-UI-Agent sets a new baseline by training and evaluating entirely on real devices, and by building safety guardrails into the agent execution loop itself. It marks a third mature real-device agent path beyond OpenAI Operator and Claude Computer Use, signaling that Chinese labs can industrialize this stack end-to-end, accelerating agentization in office, customer service, and local-services scenarios.

月之暗面 Kimi 发布 Agent Swarm：K2.5 可调动 100 子智能体并行执行 1,500 次工具调用
Moonshot releases Kimi Agent Swarm: K2.5 can deploy up to 100 sub-agents in parallel and execute 1,500 tool calls

8 月 20 日，月之暗面（Moonshot AI）将其 Kimi Agent Swarm 作为"早期研究预览"开放给顶级订阅用户。Swarm 让 K2.5 模型能拆解任务并自主创建最多 100 个子智能体并行工作，单任务最多执行 1,500+ 次工具调用，整体任务完成速度达到串行执行的 4.5 倍。

官方推荐三大场景：1）发现类（如 100 个垂直 YouTube 域名的头部创作者搜索）；2）规模输出类（如整合 40 篇 PDF 形成 100 页双栏文献综述）；3）观点博弈类（让多角色的"专家小组"对同一商业决策给出冲突性结论）。Moonshot 表示将逐步引入子智能体间直接通信与并行宽度动态控制。

On August 20, Moonshot AI opened its Kimi Agent Swarm as an early research preview to top-tier subscribers. Swarm lets K2.5 decompose tasks, autonomously spawn up to 100 sub-agents working in parallel, execute 1,500+ tool calls per task, and reach completion 4.5× faster than sequential execution.

Three recommended use cases: (1) Discovery at Scale (e.g., surveying top creators across 100 niche YouTube domains); (2) Output at Scale (e.g., synthesizing 40 social-psychology PDFs into a 100-page two-column lit review); (3) Perspective at Scale (using expert-persona sub-agents to surface conflicting views on a decision). Moonshot says it will add sub-agent communication and dynamic parallelism control.

Why it matters / 为何重要
Agent Swarm 把"多智能体协作"从研究 demo 推进到生产形态，并明确以"对抗群体思维"为产品价值。它意味着 Kimi 在 Coding/Dev Agent 之外，押注"调研/写作类多角色 Agent"作为下一个差异化战场，与 Anthropic/OpenAI 的"单智能体强化"路线形成对照。
Agent Swarm moves multi-agent collaboration from research demos to production, explicitly marketed as a hedge against groupthink. Outside Coding/Dev Agents, Kimi is now betting on "research/writing-style multi-role agents" as its next differentiator — a deliberate contrast with Anthropic/OpenAI's single-agent scaling path.

月之暗面 WorldVQA 基准发布：3,500 张图像问答，专测多模态模型的"世界知识幻觉"
Moonshot releases WorldVQA: a 3,500-prompt benchmark targeting world-knowledge hallucinations in multimodal LLMs

8 月 20 日，月之暗面推出 WorldVQA，一个评估多模态大模型"事实性视觉世界知识"的开放基准与数据集，覆盖 9 个类别、共 3,500 对经过核验的图文问答。每道题只接受单一、可验证的真实答案，排除主观或歧义场景。

官方在 SOTA 模型上的测试显示，即使最强多模态模型在长尾视觉知识上准确率常常低于 50%——意味着模型可能只是基于视觉模式"幻觉式回答"，而非真正识别实体。基准发布旨在推动"更可信、更具世界知识储备的多模态 AI"发展。

On August 20, Moonshot released WorldVQA, an open benchmark and dataset for evaluating multimodal LLMs' factual visual world knowledge. It covers 9 categories with 3,500 verified image-question pairs, each with a single, verifiable ground-truth answer and no subjective or ambiguous cases.

Tests on frontier multimodal models show even best-in-class systems often fall below 50% on long-tail visual knowledge — suggesting models may hallucinate based on visual patterns rather than truly recognizing the entity. The dataset is intended to push toward more factually reliable, knowledgeable multimodal AI.

Why it matters / 为何重要
当前主流多模态评测（如 MMMU、POPE）难以区分"真正识别"与"视觉模式幻觉"。WorldVQA 通过强制可验证答案和长尾实体覆盖，把"事实性"作为评测维度独立化，可能成为未来 VLM 选型与企业部署的常用过滤器。
Mainstream multimodal benchmarks like MMMU and POPE cannot reliably distinguish "true recognition" from "visual-pattern hallucination." By enforcing verifiable answers and long-tail entity coverage, WorldVQA isolates factuality as a first-class axis — likely becoming a routine filter for VLM selection and enterprise deployment.

Anthropic's August Risk Report reveals internal "Model 2": 1.5 AECI points above Mythos 5, withheld pending safety testing

本月（8 月 14 日发布）Anthropic 第二期全公司《Risk Report》首次披露代号"Model 2"的内部模型：归属 Mythos 级别，在内部能力指数 AECI 上比 Claude Mythos 5 高约 1.5 分；在内部 CoBench（449 道历史 R&D 题目）上得分 62.8%，对比 Mythos 5 的 50.3%。该模型已广泛用于编码、数据生成与研究/工程任务，许多任务通过"持续运行的 Agent"完成；Claude 各版本已经撰写 Anthropic 生产系统大部分代码。

Model 2 在部署前仅做了"不及 Mythos 5 严格"的内部审查，未发现更严重错位信号，因此整体错位风险被自评为"低"。但 Anthropic 表示"无当前外部发布计划"。同一份报告同时披露：1）公司在高风险场景的灾难性错位风险评级从"很低"上调至"低"；2）生物武器相关分类器在 2025 年 5 月至 2026 年 4 月期间因调试标志被静默禁用 11 个月，涉及约 1.33 亿次交互；3）公司承认其任务型能力评估已"饱和"，新指标 CoBench 也在逼近上限。

In its second company-wide Risk Report (published August 14), Anthropic disclosed an internal model called "Model 2." It sits in the Mythos tier, scores about 1.5 AECI points above Claude Mythos 5, and reaches 62.8% on CoBench (449 historical R&D problems) versus Mythos 5's 50.3%. It already powers coding, data generation, and research/engineering workloads — often via continuously running agents — and Claude systems now write most code inside Anthropic's production.

Model 2 went through internal review less rigorous than Mythos 5's. Anthropic found no new or more concerning misalignment signals and rates overall misalignment risk as "low," but states "no current plans" to release it. The same report: (1) raises catastrophic-misalignment risk in high-stakes settings from "very low" to "low"; (2) discloses that bioweapon-safeguard classifiers were silently disabled for 11 months (May 2025–April 2026) via a debug flag, affecting ~133M exchanges; (3) admits task-based capability evaluations have saturated, with CoBench itself approaching ceiling.

Why it matters / 为何重要
"Model 2" 披露与 11 个月的安全护栏盲区是 Anthropic 主动"自爆"安全史上的最大单一文档。它把"前沿实验室最先部署的总是自家未公开模型"从暗箱明文化，且揭示了一个结构性矛盾：能力评估饱和与安全检查被动降级正同步发生。
The "Model 2" disclosure, paired with the 11-month safeguard blind spot, is the largest single document of Anthropic self-disclosed safety history. It formalizes a previously implicit fact — frontier labs deploy unreleased models first — and surfaces a structural contradiction: capability evaluations saturate while safety checks quietly degrade.

Penn State：上下文压缩平均仅 17% 的会话级指令能"幸存"，Agent 长程可靠性被显著低估
Penn State: context compression silently drops ~83% of session-level instructions, undermining long-horizon agent reliability

8 月 19 日 The Decoder 报道 Penn State 一项系统化测量：当 Agent 系统对历史上下文做压缩（compaction）以管理 token 预算时，"会话级约束"——例如"先确认再修改"、"回复中不要使用我的名字"等指令——是最先被牺牲的内容，平均仅 17% 能跨过压缩保留下来。

研究指出，会话级指令既不属于任务本身、也不属于永久系统提示，正好落在压缩器"为任务连续性服务"的优化盲区中。一个小型"约束守门员"LLM 即可恢复大部分损失。结论：长时 Agent 系统的真实风险比当前基准所示更高——你"以为它记住了"的规则可能早在三小时前的自动压缩中被丢弃。

Reported by The Decoder on August 19, Penn State researchers systematically measured what AI systems lose when they compress ("compact") context to manage token budgets. "Session-level constraints" — instructions like "confirm with me before making changes" or "never use my name in replies" — were the first to go: on average only 17% survive.

The work shows these rules sit between the task itself and permanent system prompts, falling into the blind spot of compressors optimized for task continuity. A small "constraint-guardian" LLM recovers most of the loss. For long-horizon agents, the rule you assume is remembered may have disappeared in an automatic compaction three hours ago.

Why it matters / 为何重要
该研究为 Agent 工程提出了"压缩即丢规则"的工程警告，并暗示一种低成本外挂守门员的可能架构。它配合 Anthropic"Agent 评估饱和"、OpenAI StateM 等结论，把"Agent 工程从'给得越多越好'转向'给得刚好'"作为下一阶段行业共识。
The study raises an engineering red flag — "compression eats rules first" — and sketches a cheap guardian-LLM architecture. Combined with Anthropic's "agent evals have saturated" and the StateM harness work, it cements a near-term consensus: agent engineering is shifting from "give it more" to "give it exactly enough."

Qwen3.8-27B GGUF 正式发布：Unsloth Dynamic V3 量化，准确率比同类提升 10%，8GB RAM 可跑 1-bit 版
Qwen3.8-27B GGUF released: Unsloth Dynamic V3 quantization lifts accuracy by 10%, 1-bit version runs on 8GB RAM
