Wall Street shares notch records as oil prices stabilize, bond yields retreat
美股再创收盘新高：油价企稳、美债收益率回落提振市场情绪
World stocks scaled two-week highs on Tuesday with Wall Street indexes setting new records, as steady oil prices and falling bond yields boosted sentiment. The S&P 500 hit a fresh record, up about 0.6%, while the Nasdaq gained 0.45%, also an all-time high. The Dow Jones rose 0.5%. Investors looked ahead to an earnings season expected to be powered by AI-driven growth, with Goldman Sachs estimating 27% S&P 500 earnings growth. Long-dated US Treasury yields eased after touching fresh 24-year highs, with the 10-year yield falling 2.7 bps to 5.28%. Traders scaled back expectations of a Fed rate increase this month to 19% from about 50% a week earlier.
周二全球股市触及两周高点，华尔街三大指数齐创收盘新高：标普500指数上涨约0.6%，纳斯达克指数涨0.45%同创历史新高，道琼斯指数涨0.5%。油价企稳与美债收益率回落提振市场情绪，投资者憧憬由AI驱动的强劲三季度财报季，高盛预计标普500盈利同比增长约27%。美国10年期国债收益率从24年高位回落2.7个基点至5.28%，市场对美联储本月加息的预期从一周前的约50%降至19%。

Federal Reserve to release September FOMC meeting minutes on October 7
美联储将于10月7日公布9月FOMC会议纪要
The Federal Reserve is scheduled to publish the minutes of the September 15-16 FOMC meeting at 2:00 p.m. ET on Wednesday, October 7, 2026. The meeting resulted in a 25-basis-point rate increase to a target range of 3.75%-4.00%. reuters.com⭐⭐⭐⭐
US EIA hikes oil price forecasts again as Iran war drains global stockpile
美国能源信息署再度上调油价预测：伊朗局势导致全球库存快速下降
The US Energy Information Administration raised its oil price forecast for this year and next year on Tuesday, as global stockpiles fall rapidly and diesel markets remain tight due to the ongoing Iran war. Global benchmark Brent crude is now expected to average about $98 a barrel in 2026, up 8% from the previous forecast. Brent is forecast to average about $105 a barrel in the fourth quarter, $14 above the prior estimate. However, Middle East oil production and exports are expected to gradually recover as transit through the Strait of Hormuz improves and producers shift to alternative export routes. As flows recover, Brent is expected to average $84 a barrel in 2027.
美国能源信息署（EIA）周二上调了2026年及2027年油价预测，因伊朗战争导致全球石油库存快速下降、柴油市场持续紧张。全球基准布伦特原油2026年均值预计约为每桶98美元，较此前预测上调8%；第四季度均价预计约为105美元，较此前高出14美元。不过，随着霍尔木兹海峡通航改善以及产油国转向替代出口路线，中东石油产量和出口有望逐步恢复，2027年布伦特均价预计回落至每桶84美元。

Suspected Iranian drone plot led US to withdraw bombers from UK airbase
疑遭伊朗无人机袭击威胁，美国从英国空军基地撤出轰炸机
A suspected plot involving Iran to launch a complex drone attack on a key British airbase led the United States to remove its B-1 bomber aircraft from RAF Fairford, two US officials told Reuters on Tuesday. The base is the preferred European base for US bombers. Iran has denied any involvement. British police arrested a 22-year-old British man in London on suspicion of preparing terrorist acts as part of the investigation. The incident signals continued geopolitical tensions even as oil flows through the Strait of Hormuz have shown signs of recovery.
两名美国官员周二告诉路透社，疑似伊朗策划对英国一处关键空军基地发动复杂无人机袭击，促使美国将其B-1轰炸机从费尔福德皇家空军基地撤离；该基地是美国轰炸机在欧洲的首选驻地。伊朗否认与此事有关。英国警方在伦敦逮捕了一名22岁英国男子，涉嫌为调查中的恐怖袭击做准备。这一事件表明，尽管霍尔木兹海峡石油运输出现复苏迹象，地缘政治紧张局势仍在持续。

Asian shares mostly advance after Wall Street gains and oil prices fall
亚股多数上涨：追随美股涨势，油价回落
Asian shares were mostly higher on Tuesday and U.S. futures advanced after shares rose to near a record close on Wall Street. US stocks flirt with a record after oil prices ease
油价回落推动美股逼近历史最高纪录
U.S. stocks are rising Tuesday and on track to hit an all-time high after oil prices gave back some of their big recent gains. The S&P 500 added 0.5% and was on pace to finish the day above its record set in August. The Dow Jones was up 185 points, or 0.4%, and the Nasdaq was adding 0.6% to its own all-time high.
