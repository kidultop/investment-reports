apnews.com⭐⭐⭐
US hiring slows to 29,000 in September, unemployment ticks up to 4.2% ahead of midterm elections
美国9月非农仅增2.9万、失业率升至4.2%，中期选举前就业明显降温
U.S. employers added a disappointing 29,000 jobs in September and the unemployment rate rose to 4.2% from 4.1%, the Labor Department reported Friday - the last jobs report before the Nov. 3 midterm elections. July and August payrolls were revised down by a combined 60,000 jobs. Average hourly wages rose just 3% from a year earlier, the smallest year-over-year gain since May 2021, a cooling that may encourage the Fed to keep its key rate unchanged when it meets next month rather than raise it again.
美国劳工部周五公布：9月非农新增就业仅2.9万，远低于市场预期的约9万；失业率由4.1%升至4.2%；7-8月数据合计下修6万。时薪同比仅增3%，为2021年5月以来最小增幅。这是11月3日中期选举前的最后一份就业报告——雇佣降温或促使美联储下月按兵不动而非继续加息。

Oil prices drop as Europe agrees to release diesel reserves under G7 coordinated plan
G7协调释储落地：欧洲同意释放柴油储备，国际油价下跌
Oil prices fell about $2 on Friday after European leaders agreed to a request by President Trump to release diesel from their reserves. EU governments acted on a French proposal for Europe to release 50 million barrels of diesel and for IEA members to release 50 million barrels of crude over four months, with diesel front-loaded in the first 20 days. Brent settled at $102.25 a barrel (December contract, -0.06%) and WTI at $91.11 (-1.90%). Barclays raised its Q4 Brent forecast by $20 to $115, citing strong physical fundamentals despite recovering Middle East flows.
在特朗普施压下，欧洲领导人周五同意释放柴油储备压低油价：法国提议欧洲释放5,000万桶柴油、IEA成员国释放5,000万桶原油，4个月内完成且柴油在前20天前置释放。布伦特12月合约结算102.25美元（-0.06%）、WTI 11月合约91.11美元（-1.90%）。巴克莱仍将Q4布伦特预测上调20美元至115美元——柴油结构性短缺与中东供应风险是主要上行变量。
