White House Signs Voluntary AI Accord: Trump and Six Tech Giants Commit to Four-Layer Controls and Independent Audits
Policy US
当地时间 9 月 29 日，美国总统特朗普在白宫与 Anthropic、Google、Meta、OpenAI、NVIDIA、xAI/SpaceX 等六大前沿 AI 公司首席执行官签署《白宫超级智能协议：前沿责任联合承诺》。该文件为一页自愿性协议，要求企业建立四层管控与审计机制：强有力的内部控制与监测、内部团队保障整改、独立外部审计或评估、董事会独立委员会监督。协议重点关注网络安全、生物安全和化学威胁风险，并防止模型以非预期方式入侵技术系统。特朗普称该协议"具有道德约束力"，并签署行政令要求联邦机构以"超级智能"（Super Intelligence）替代"人工智能"表述。批评者指出协议缺乏法律强制力与处罚机制，马斯克讽其为"互相批改作业"。
On September 29, President Trump signed the "White House Accord on Super Intelligence: Joint Commitment on Frontier Responsibilities" with CEOs from Anthropic, Google, Meta, OpenAI, NVIDIA, and xAI/SpaceX. The one-page voluntary accord requires four layers of controls and audits: robust internal controls and monitoring, internal teams to ensure remediation, independent external audits or evaluations, and board-level independent committee oversight. It focuses on cyber, biological, and chemical threat risks and preventing models from accessing technical systems in unintended ways. Trump called the accord "morally binding" and signed an executive order directing federal agencies to use "Super Intelligence" instead of "artificial intelligence." arXiv 论文 PreviewDiff：多模态评论家引导扩散隐空间搜索，提升图像视频生成提示遵循度
arXiv Paper PreviewDiff: Multimodal Critic-Guided Search over Diffusion Latents Improves Prompt Faithfulness
Research Open
MIT 等机构研究者 9 月 28 日在 arXiv 发表论文 PreviewDiff（arXiv:2609.36199），提出一种无需训练的测试时搜索方法。该方法在扩散模型去噪过程中的选定检查点解码部分预览，由多模态评判器打分并给出自然语言反馈，再基于语义提示编辑和局部重噪声化的隐空间分支进行搜索。在图像和视频生成基准上，PreviewDiff 持续优于同等计算预算的 Best-of-N 选择和标量搜索基线。消融实验表明，更早干预和增加搜索宽度带来的收益最大。
Researchers from MIT and other institutions published PreviewDiff on arXiv on September 28 (arXiv:2609.36199), proposing a training-free test-time search method. At selected denoising checkpoints, it decodes a partial preview, asks a multimodal critic to score and critique it, and uses natural-language feedback to branch over semantic prompt edits and locally re-noised latent continuations. Across image and video generation benchmarks, PreviewDiff consistently improves over budget-matched Best-of-N selection and strong scalar-search baselines. Ablations show earlier interventions and increased search width provide the largest gains.
为何重要 Why it matters： PreviewDiff 将验证器计算从"事后筛选"转变为"生成过程中的主动控制器"，有望改善扩散模型在物体数量、属性绑定和空间关系等组合细节上的提示遵循能力，而不需要重新训练模型。/ PreviewDiff turns verifier compute from "post-hoc selection" into an "active controller inside the generation process," potentially improving diffusion models' prompt adherence on compositional details like object counts, attribute binding, and spatial relations without retraining.

arXiv 论文解析循环语言模型：何时循环有效、如何分配层与迭代
arXiv Paper Analyzes Looped Language Models: When Recurrence Helps and How to Allocate Layers and Iterations
Research Open
香港中文大学等机构研究者 9 月 29 日在 arXiv 发表论文（arXiv:2609.36636），系统研究循环语言模型（LoopLM）——通过参数共享增加计算深度、在不增加参数的前提下扩展推理计算。研究发现：循环可提升训练范围之外的推理能力，但会损害知识性能；更难的推理实例并未一致受益。有效深度本身不足以预测行为，非循环输出层可改善 under-unrolling 鲁棒性，输入输出层的最优位置随推理预算变化。作者提出 history-state injection 结合 timestep conditioning 的设计，以在扩展展开时更好地保持知识并提升跨预算鲁棒性。
Researchers from The Chinese University of Hong Kong and others published a paper on arXiv on September 29 (arXiv:2609.36636) systematically studying Looped Language Models (LoopLMs), which increase computational depth through parameter sharing to scale inference compute without adding parameters. They find recurrence can improve reasoning beyond the training horizon while degrading knowledge performance; harder reasoning instances do not consistently benefit more. Effective depth alone is insufficient to predict behavior. Non-recurrent output layers improve robustness to under-unrolling, while preferred placement of input/output layers varies with inference budget. The authors propose history-state injection combined with timestep conditioning to better preserve knowledge under extended unrolling and improve robustness across budgets.
为何重要 Why it matters： LoopLM 是提升推理效率的重要方向之一。该研究为循环架构的设计提供了可操作的指导原则，帮助研究者在不增加模型参数的情况下，根据任务类型和推理预算优化循环策略。/ LoopLMs are an important direction for improving inference efficiency. This research provides actionable design guidelines for recurrent architectures, helping researchers optimize recurrence strategies based on task type and inference budget without adding model parameters.

Hugging Face 推出开放 TTS 排行榜：客观指标评估多语言语音合成与语音克隆
Hugging Face Launches Open TTS Leaderboard: Objective Metrics for Multilingual TTS and Voice Cloning
Open
Hugging Face 于 9 月 30 日发布 Open TTS Leaderboard，旨在用可复现的客观指标评估多语言文本转语音（TTS）和语音克隆模型。截至发布时，Hugging Face Hub 上已有超过 8,000 个 TTS 模型，但评估方法分散且难以规模化。新排行榜使用 WER/CER（基于 Qwen3 ASR）、逆实时因子（RTFx）、首音时间（TTFA）和说话人相似度（SIM）等指标，可在数小时内完成模型评估，而不需要 arena 投票所需的数周。排行榜特别关注开源模型和多语言覆盖，评估脚本将开源。
Hugging Face launched the Open TTS Leaderboard on September 30, aiming to provide reproducible objective metrics for multilingual text-to-speech and voice-cloning models. As of launch, there are over 8,000 TTS models on the Hugging Face Hub, but evaluation methods remain fragmented and hard to scale. The new leaderboard uses WER/CER (via Qwen3 ASR), inverse real-time factor (RTFx), time-to-first-audio (TTFA), and speaker similarity (SIM), enabling model evaluation in hours rather than the weeks required for arena voting. It focuses particularly on open-weight models and multilingual coverage, with evaluation scripts to be open-sourced.

arXiv Paper Proposes "Memory as Middleware": Pluggable Memory Layer for Self-Improving AI Agents
Open Research
IBM 等机构研究者 9 月 25 日在 arXiv 发表论文（arXiv:2609.32091），提出 AI 代理的记忆应成为一类独立的中间件，而非每个代理各自手写的专属逻辑。论文指出当前 bespoke memory 导致记忆无法跨代理交换、共享、隔离或独立治理，并定义了六大系统挑战：双侧可插拔性、宿主原生拦截、多租户隔离、写路径一致性、带溯源的联邦共享和生命周期治理。作者提出参考实现 ALTK-Evolve，为自改进代理提供可插拔的记忆中间件。
Researchers from IBM and other institutions published a paper on arXiv on September 25 (arXiv:2609.32091) arguing that AI agent memory should become a standalone middleware layer rather than bespoke hand-wired logic for each agent. They note that current bespoke memory creates fragmentation where memory cannot be swapped, shared, isolated, or governed independently, and define six systems challenges: two-sided pluggability, host-native interposition, multi-tenant isolation, write-path consistency, federated sharing with provenance, and lifecycle governance. The authors present ALTK-Evolve, a reference implementation of memory middleware for self-improving agents.
