OpenAI 设立独立数学与人工智能顾问组：内部模型已解 Navier–Stokes 等百余个开放问题2026-09-21
OpenAI Establishes Independent Advisory Group on Mathematics and AI as Internal Model Resolves Navier–Stokes and 100+ Open Problems
OpenAI 宣布，其 8 月 28 日开始训练的新内部模型除解决 Navier–Stokes 千年 prize 问题外，已解决涵盖多数数学领域的 100 余个长期悬而未决的问题，进展速度令内部数学家感到意外。为回应数学界对「以开放问题作为基准」负外部性的公开信（《A Severe Misalignment of AI in Mathematics》），OpenAI 与数学家共同设立独立顾问组，托管于普林斯顿高等研究院（IAS），成员包括 Timothy Gowers、Martin Hairer、Edward Witten、Melanie Matchett Wood 等九位数学家。顾问组独立于 OpenAI 运作、成员不受薪，可公开发表意见，负责评估成果重要性、协调发布节奏与学术规范，但不会就 OpenAI 内部研究进度提出建议。
OpenAI says a new internal model, training since Aug 28, has resolved the Navier–Stokes Millennium Prize problem plus over 100 long-standing open problems across most areas of mathematics. Responding to an open letter from mathematicians, OpenAI is working with an independent Advisory Group on Mathematics and AI, hosted at the Institute for Advanced Study, with members including Timothy Gowers, Martin Hairer, Edward Witten and Melanie Matchett Wood. The group operates independently, is unpaid by OpenAI, and will advise on significance assessment, dissemination and academic standards.
为何重要 Why it matters：这是官方确认的 AI 数学能力里程碑，同时展示了「外部独立监督」机制如何在实际能力跃迁前介入——顾问组的独立性设计（不受薪、可公开异议）可能成为其他实验室处理学科冲击的参照模板。/ An officially confirmed milestone in AI mathematics capability, and a live test of whether independent external oversight can keep pace with frontier capability jumps — the group's independence design may become a template for other labs.
Official confirmation that AI is compressing decades of open-problem work into months, paired with a novel governance mechanism.

GVPO++：把 KL 约束奖励最大化的解析解引入后训练与在策略蒸馏2026-09-21
GVPO++: Group Variance Policy Optimization for LLM Post-Training and On-Policy Distillation (NeurIPS 2025 Extension)
GVPO 的扩展版本更新至 arXiv：该方法将 KL 约束奖励最大化的解析解直接融入梯度加权，避免了 GRPO 类方法对重要性采样的依赖及其带来的训练不稳定，保证唯一最优解，并自然扩展到在策略蒸馏（OPD）的一族目标。作者称其为「可靠后训练」提供了理论保证与工程灵活性兼得的范式。
The extended GVPO++ (arXiv, extending a NeurIPS 2025 paper) embeds the analytical solution of KL-constrained reward maximization into gradient weighting, removing the importance-sampling instability of GRPO-style methods, guaranteeing a unique optimum, and extending naturally to a family of on-policy distillation objectives.
为何重要 Why it matters：RL 后训练的稳定性是各家实验室的真实工程痛点；有解析保证、无需重要性采样的替代目标若被大规模验证，会直接影响下一代开源训练框架（如 slime、OpenRLHF）的默认配置。/ A stable, provably-optimal alternative to GRPO could reset defaults in open RL post-training frameworks.
Post-training stability is an engineering pain point everywhere; analytic guarantees matter.
