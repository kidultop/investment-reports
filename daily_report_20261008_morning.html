India RBI hikes repo rate to 5.50%, first increase in nearly four years, shifts stance to calibrated tightening
印度央行近四年首次加息：回购利率上调至5.50%，立场转为渐进式紧缩
The Reserve Bank of India raised its benchmark repo rate by 25 basis points to 5.50% on Wednesday, the first rise in nearly four years, as rising inflation, a weakening rupee and elevated crude oil prices from the West Asia conflict prompt a policy pivot. The monetary policy committee unanimously hiked while shifting its stance to 'calibrated tightening' in a 4:2 vote, ruling out near-term rate cuts; the RBI raised its FY27 inflation forecast to 5.2% and lifted its GDP growth forecast to 7.1%. Economists at Capital Economics expect 25bp hikes in December and February toward a 6% peak, while HDFC Bank sees another 50-75 bps of tightening. India's Nifty 50 fell 0.76% and the Sensex lost 0.59% after the decision.
印度储备银行（RBI）周三将基准回购利率上调25个基点至5.50%，为近四年来首次加息；通胀上升、卢比走弱及西亚冲突推高的原油价格促使政策转向。货币政策委员会一致投票加息，并以4:2将立场从'中性'调整为'渐进式紧缩'，短期降息已无可能；RBI将2026-27财年通胀预测上调至5.2%、GDP增长预测上调至7.1%。资本经济学预计12月和2月各加息25bp至6%峰值，HDFC银行预计还有50-75bp紧缩。决议公布后印度Nifty 50跌0.76%、Sensex跌0.59%。
federalreserve.gov⭐⭐⭐
Fed minutes: September rate hike unanimous, most officials see further interest rate increase by year-end as appropriate
美联储9月纪要：加息获一致支持，多数官员认为年底前再次加息可能是合适的
Minutes of the September 15-16 FOMC meeting show the Committee unanimously voted to raise the federal funds interest rate target range by 25 basis points to 3.75%-4.00%, its first hike since July 2023. Most participants judged it would likely be appropriate to raise the target range further by year-end, while stressing decisions stay data dependent. Inflation remains elevated with limited progress toward the 2% goal: geopolitics has pushed up crude oil and fuel prices, and a surge in AI-related investment adds to inflation pressure. Some officials favored patience with a possible December move, pointing to an October pause, with attention turning to the October 27-28 FOMC meeting.
9月15-16日FOMC会议纪要显示，委员会一致同意将联邦基金利率目标区间上调25个基点至3.75%-4.00%，为2023年7月以来首次加息。多数与会者认为年底前进一步加息可能是合适的，同时强调未来决定取决于数据。通胀仍居高不下、向2%目标的进展有限：地缘政治推高原油及成品油价格，AI相关投资激增也加剧通胀压力。部分官员倾向保持耐心、12月再行动，10月大概率按兵不动，焦点转向10月27-28日会议。

Oil prices settle lower as IEA agrees to accelerate crude stock release and prioritize diesel; Brent holds $100
IEA同意加快释放石油储备并优先柴油，油价收跌但布伦特守住100美元
Brent crude futures settled down 0.38% at $100.20 a barrel and WTI fell 1.3% to $88.28 after the International Energy Agency agreed to speed up oil stock releases and prioritize diesel to curb record-high fuel prices as the Iran war squeezes global supplies. Completing previously announced releases could bring around 100 million barrels to market, though analysts noted this was not a fresh intervention of that size. France will release 10 million barrels of diesel. US crude inventories fell 3.2 million barrels versus expectations for a build, and a storm in the Gulf of Mexico is set to become the first Atlantic hurricane of 2026, threatening oil and gas facilities. Yemen's Houthis attacked Aden airport and Ukraine struck two Russian oil facilities, keeping supply risks elevated.
布伦特原油期货结算价跌0.38%报每桶100.20美元，WTI跌1.3%报88.28美元。此前国际能源署（IEA）同意加快落实石油储备释放计划并优先投放柴油，以遏制伊朗战争导致全球供应紧张下的创纪录燃料价格。若完成此前承诺的释放，约有1亿桶石油可投放市场（分析人士指出并非新增 intervention）。法国将释放1000万桶柴油。EIA数据显示美国原油库存意外下降320万桶；墨西哥湾风暴即将升级为2026年首个大西洋飓风，威胁油气设施。也门胡塞武装袭击亚丁机场、乌克兰打击俄两处石油设施，供应风险仍高。
