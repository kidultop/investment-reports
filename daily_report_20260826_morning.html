Druckenmiller Blasts Bond Intervention as 'Mistake'; Warns Attempt to Suppress 30-Year Yield Will Only Delay U.S. Fiscal Reckoning
Hedge-fund legend Stanley Druckenmiller — Treasury Secretary Bessent's longtime mentor — published an op-ed in the Wall Street Journal titled 'Let the Bond Market Speak' criticizing Bessent's plan to spend more than $4 billion buying long-dated Treasuries. Druckenmiller warned that rising budget deficits, debt crossing $40 trillion, and persistent inflation are the root causes of higher borrowing costs. Forcing down the 30-year yield will only postpone America's fiscal reckoning.
对冲基金巨头 Stanley Druckenmiller(财长 Bessent 的长期导师)在 WSJ 评论版发表署名文章《Let the Bond Market Speak》批评 Bessent 计划花 40 亿美元以上回购长期国债之举为「根本性错误」。Druckenmiller 警告:不断攀升的预算赤字、国债突破 40 万亿美元和持续通胀才是借贷成本上升的根本原因;强行压低 30 年期收益率只会推迟美国「财政清算」,最终不得不改革社保等社会福利
WSJ⭐⭐⭐⭐
U.S. Avoids Ruffling China's Feathers With Iran Sanctions — ING Calls Move 'Marginal, Not Market-Moving' as Oil Drops
Treasury Secretary Bessent unveiled the 'Operation Economic Outcast' Iran sanctions package on Monday but did not name China — the buyer that has kept Iran's economy afloat under U.S. pressure for years. ING called the package 'marginal, not market-moving,' since China takes 90% of Iran's oil exports and is not directly covered by the new measures. WSJ commented that 'the U.S. is squeezing Iran while avoiding its biggest lifeline.' WTI fell 4.76% to $80.96 and Brent -6.94% to $85.77, on expectations of a diplomatic breakthrough.
Bessent 周一公布「Operation Economic Outcast」伊朗制裁方案,但未提及中国 — 这正是多年来支撑伊朗经济度过美方压力的关键。ING 评估该方案为「边际性而非市场动因」,因中国买走伊朗 90% 原油出口不受新制裁约束。WSJ 评论「美国正挤压伊朗却避免触碰其最大命脉」。WTI 油价当日下跌 4.76% 至 80.96 美元,Brent -6.94% 至 85.77 美元,因市场预期外交破局

Canada Retaliates Against Trump: $20B Tariffs on U.S. Goods, Steel & Aluminum Doubled to 50%, Effective Sept. 8
Canada's Finance Minister Champagne on Tuesday announced retaliatory tariffs of roughly C$20 billion on approximately 700 U.S. goods at rates of 15%-50%, effective September 8, in a 'dollar-for-dollar' response to Trump's 50% tariffs on Canadian wine, cement, hockey sticks and others. Steel and aluminum duties doubled to 50%. A C$7.5 billion support package covers affected businesses and workers. Trump on Tuesday suggested renaming Lake Ontario 'Lake America'.
加拿大财长 Champagne 周二宣布对约 700 项美国商品征收总价值约 200 亿美元的报复性关税,税率 15%-50%,9 月 8 日生效。这是「对等美元对美元」反击特朗普对加拿大葡萄酒、水泥、曲棍球杆等征收的 50% 关税。钢铁和铝的关税翻倍至 50%。同时推出 75 亿加元一揽子计划支持受波及企业与员工。Trump 周二建议将 Lake Ontario 改名为「Lake America」
CNBC⭐⭐⭐
Trump Targets Iran's Trade Lifelines: China, UAE, Turkey, Iraq, India Most Exposed to Secondary Sanctions Regime
The Trump administration on Monday launched an 'Economic D-Day' against Iran, threatening to cut off any entity that launders money for Iran from the U.S. dollar system. China buys roughly 90% of Iran's oil exports (independent refineries route via Malaysia / Indonesia transshipment); banks are sanctioned but refineries continue to run. The UAE became Iran's largest import source over the past year (about 30% share) and paused all trade finance last weekend after an Iranian missile attack. Eurasia Group assessed that China will not retaliate publicly but will quietly tighten compliance to avoid being pulled in.
特朗普政府周一对伊朗推出「经济 D-Day」行动,威胁切断任何为伊朗洗钱的实体接入美元体系。中国买走伊朗约 90% 原油出口(独立炼油商从马来西亚/印尼转口),银行体系受制裁但炼厂仍正常运转。UAE 过去一年是伊朗最大进口来源(占比 30%),上周末因伊朗导弹袭击暂停全部贸易金融。Eurasia Group 评估:中国不会公开反击,但会悄悄加强合规避免被卷入
