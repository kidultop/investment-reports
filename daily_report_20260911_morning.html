ECB raises interest rates as the Iran war fans inflation fears
欧央行加息应对伊朗战争推升的通胀
The European Central Bank raised interest rates for the second time this year, by a quarter point to 2.50%, seeking to quell an energy-driven rise in inflation triggered by the Iran war even as it warned fuel prices could move higher. It acknowledged that even then inflation was expected to stay above its 2% goal through to 2028. The decision prompted traders to raise bets on further hikes, pricing in 60 basis points more of increases by the April 2027 meeting, up from around 51 basis points beforehand. The ECB now sees inflation at 3.0% this year, 2.5% next year and 2.1% in 2028, and expects the euro zone economy to grow 0.9%, 1.4% and 1.5% respectively. Core inflation eased to 2.4% last month.
欧洲央行今年第二次加息，上调25个基点至2.50%，试图压制由伊朗战争引发的能源驱动型通胀，同时警告燃料价格可能进一步走高。该行承认，即便如此，通胀预计仍将在2028年之前高于2%的目标。决议公布后交易员加大了对进一步加息的押注，定价到2027年4月会议前还将加息60个基点，高于此前的约51个基点。欧央行目前预计今年通胀3.0%、明年2.5%、2028年2.1%；欧元区经济增速分别为0.9%、1.4%和1.5%。上月核心通胀回落至2.4%。

Lagarde says the ECB has not debated any future rate path
拉加德：欧央行完全没有讨论未来利率路径
ECB President Christine Lagarde stressed the bank did not pre-commit to any future moves. "We have not debated at all any kind of future path," she told a press conference. "Markets do what they have to do and we do what we have to do — which is to provide price stability." She said the risks to the inflation outlook are to the upside, due in particular to the Middle East conflict, and that the energy shock could intensify further with effects on other prices and wages stronger than expected. Gas prices could rise on further supply disruptions or an unusually cold winter with low storage. The ECB's wage tracker points to a modest uptick to 2.7% in negotiated wage growth in the first half of 2027. Economists polled by Reuters had expected the hike to be the ECB's last for now, though some shifted their view after the meeting.
欧洲央行行长拉加德强调该行不预先承诺任何未来行动。「我们完全没有讨论任何形式的未来路径，」她在新闻发布会上表示，「市场做市场该做的事，我们做我们该做的事——那就是提供价格稳定。」她表示，通胀前景风险偏上行，主要源于中东冲突，能源冲击可能进一步加剧，对其他价格与工资的影响可能强于预期。若出现进一步的供应中断，或异常寒冷冬季叠加低库存，天然气价格可能上涨。欧央行工资追踪指标显示，2027年上半年协议工资增速将温和升至2.7%。路透调查的经济学家此前预计本次加息是欧央行近期最后一次，但会后部分机构已改变看法。

Wall Street dips as oil holds above $100 before US inflation data
美股走低，油价守住100美元，市场静待美国通胀数据
Nervy markets were waiting for key U.S. inflation data after the ECB lifted rates and oil held above $100 a barrel following U.S. and Iranian tanker missile strikes in the Strait of Hormuz. Brent crude climbed above $100 for the first time since July, raising fears of renewed inflation pressure just as bond yields in major economies hover near multi-decade highs. Wall Street opened lower again with the Dow down 0.33%, the S&P 500 dropping 0.56% and the Nasdaq Composite falling 0.97%, while MSCI's gauge of stocks worldwide was down 0.69%. Investors were also digesting signs of looser U.S. fiscal policy after President Donald Trump promised to pay every U.S. adult a $5,000 dividend if his party wins November's congressional elections. A majority of economists polled by Reuters expect the Fed to hold rates steady next week.
在欧央行加息、以及美伊在霍尔木兹海峡互相以导弹袭击油轮导致油价守住每桶100美元上方之后，紧张的市场正在等待关键的美国通胀数据。布伦特原油7月以来首次升破100美元，在主要经济体债券收益率徘徊于数十年高位之际，引发了对通胀压力重现的担忧。华尔街再度低开，道指跌0.33%、标普500跌0.56%、纳斯达克综合指数跌0.97%，MSCI全球股指下跌0.69%。投资者同时消化美国财政政策放松的信号——特朗普承诺若其政党赢得11月国会选举，将向每位美国成年人发放5,000美元「分红」。路透调查的多数经济学家预计美联储下周将按兵不动。

US producer inflation tops expectations as diesel costs jump
美国8月PPI超预期上行，柴油成本飙升是主因
US wholesale inflation picked up more than expected in August, driven by higher energy costs, particularly diesel, as the war with Iran continued. The Producer Price Index was up 5.4% year-on-year, a significant acceleration from July's 4.8% rise and above the 5.3% economists expected. On a month-on-month basis PPI rose 0.4%, in line with expectations. Energy costs jumped 4.2% over the month, boosted by diesel fuel, which surged 24.1%. US diesel now stands at a national average of $5.98 per gallon, a record, against a year-ago average of $3.71, according to AAA. The CME Group's FedWatch tool puts the likelihood of a 25 basis point rate hike at nearly 70% for next week's Federal Reserve meeting. Nationwide senior economist Ben Ayers said it is likely to be a close call on whether the FOMC raises rates.
受能源成本（尤其是柴油）上涨推动，美国8月批发通胀升幅超出预期。生产者价格指数（PPI）同比上涨5.4%，较7月的4.8%明显加速，也高于经济学家预期的5.3%；环比上涨0.4%，符合预期。能源成本当月大涨4.2%，其中柴油价格飙升24.1%。据美国汽车协会（AAA）数据，美国柴油全国均价已达每加仑5.98美元的历史新高，而去年同期为3.71美元。芝加哥商品交易所FedWatch工具显示，美联储下周会议加息25个基点的概率已接近70%。Nationwide高级经济学家Ben Ayers表示，联邦公开市场委员会下周是否加息「很可能是个势均力敌的决定」。

Brent crude extends surge as tanker strikes disrupt Gulf shipping
布伦特原油续涨，油轮遇袭扰乱海湾航运
Brent crude futures jumped another 3.8% to $105 a barrel amid the re-escalation of the Iran war, with U.S. crude later breaking back above $100 for the first time since May. The moves followed U.S. and Iranian tanker missile strikes in the Strait of Hormuz, a crucial route for global energy transit. "I think that Brent pushing through the $100 level will be seen by many in the market as a significant event," said Nick Twidale, chief market strategist at ATFX Global, adding that traders who had been holding off in hopes of a Middle East peace deal may now "hit the trigger as the realities of a longer conflict kick in." Oil prices are a particular concern for fuel-importing regions such as Europe and Japan.
随着伊朗战事再度升级，布伦特原油期货再涨3.8%至每桶105美元，美国原油随后自5月以来首次重回100美元上方。此轮走势紧随美伊在霍尔木兹海峡的油轮导弹互袭——该海峡是全球能源运输的关键通道。ATFX Global首席市场策略师Nick Twidale表示：「我认为布伦特突破100美元会被市场许多人视为当前局势中的一个重大事件。」他补充说，此前寄望中东和平协议而观望的交易员，可能随着「冲突长期化的现实显现」而「扣动扳机」。油价对欧洲与日本等能源进口地区构成尤为突出的压力。

Global bond yields scale multi-year highs as inflation fears spread
全球债券收益率创多年新高，通胀担忧蔓延
After the global selloff pushed 30-year Treasury yields to their highest level since 2007, the benchmark 10-year U.S. Treasury yield ticked up to 4.91%, while the 30-year touched 5.35%. Germany's 10-year bond yield held at 3.45%, the highest since the euro zone crisis in April 2011, while France's OAT yield was at another post-2008 high of 4.35%. The UK's 10-year and 20-year yields bobbed near respective post-2007 and 1998 highs of 5.26% and 5.87%. Treasury Secretary Scott Bessent had said in August the government would increase buybacks of longer-dated bonds, but the announced $6 billion operation disappointed some investors. "He may win a battle or two, but he'll only win the war if bond traders let him," said Matt Simpson of StoneX.
在 global 抛售推动30年期美国国债收益率升至2007年以来最高水平之后，10年期美债收益率上行至4.91%，30年期触及5.35%。德国10年期国债收益率维持在3.45%，为2011年4月欧债危机以来最高；法国OAT收益率达到4.35%，为2008年后又一新高。英国10年期与20年期收益率分别在5.26%和5.87%附近，接近2007年与1998年以来的高位。美国财长贝森特曾在8月表示政府将增加长期债券回购，但宣布的60亿美元操作令部分投资者失望。StoneX的Matt Simpson表示：「他可能赢下一两场战斗，但只有当债券交易员愿意时，他才能赢下这场战争。」

Wholesale inflation hits 5.4% annually, the highest pace this year
批发通胀同比5.4%，创年内最高
The Producer Price Index rose 0.4% in August on a seasonally adjusted basis, putting the annual rate at 5.4%, the highest 12-month reading of 2026. Final demand goods advanced 1.1%, with energy up 4.2%; diesel alone accounted for more than a third of the goods increase. Final demand services edged up just 0.1%. Core PPI, excluding food and energy, rose 0.2% on the month and 4.6% from a year earlier. Further up the supply chain pressures were more intense: stage 1 intermediate demand, the earliest stage, rose 1.4% in August and 11.3% over 12 months, while processed goods for intermediate demand gained 1.8% and 11.5% annually. The portfolio management index fell 1.6% but is still 18.8% higher year-on-year.
8月生产者价格指数经季节调整后环比上涨0.4%，同比升至5.4%，为2026年最高的12个月读数。最终需求商品上涨1.1%，其中能源涨4.2%；仅柴油一项就贡献了商品涨幅的三分之一以上。最终需求服务仅微涨0.1%。剔除食品与能源的核心PPI环比涨0.2%、同比涨4.6%。供应链更上游的压力更为显著：处于最早阶段的第一阶段中间需求8月环比涨1.4%、同比涨11.3%；加工商品中间需求环比涨1.8%、同比涨11.5%。投资组合管理指数8月下跌1.6%，但同比仍高出18.8%。
