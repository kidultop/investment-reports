Experiments show significant reductions in jailbreak success across red-teaming benchmarks while preserving performance on benign tasks.
为何重要： 在模型权重日益开放、越狱手段层出不穷的背景下，"推理时安全"提供了一条不依赖完整微调的防御路径，对开源与闭源模型都具备吸引力。
Why it matters: As model weights become more open and jailbreak techniques multiply, inference-time safety offers a defense path that does not require full fine-tuning, appealing to both open and closed model providers.
