Oil Jumps 2% as Saudi Attacks and Tanker Strikes Revive Hormuz Fears
沙特遇袭及油轮遭打击，油价跳涨2%，霍尔木兹海峡担忧重燃
Oil prices climbed to their highest levels in nearly two weeks on Monday morning as fresh attacks on Saudi Arabia and oil tankers near the Strait of Hormuz put Middle East supply risks back at the center of the market. West Texas Intermediate was trading at $93.78 per barrel, up $1.93, or 2.10%, while Brent crude rose $2.09, or 2.00%, to $106.80 per barrel. The renewed buying followed a weekend of attacks on Saudi Arabia, including a Houthi strike on Riyadh's international airport on Saturday that killed 13 people and wounded more than 300. Goldman Sachs raised its December Brent forecast to $90 per barrel from $85, estimating that geopolitical risks are currently adding just over $20 per barrel to oil prices.
周一上午，沙特遭遇新一轮袭击以及霍尔木兹海峡附近油轮遭打击，中东供应风险重回市场焦点，油价攀升至近两周最高水平。WTI原油报93.78美元/桶，上涨1.93美元或2.10%；布伦特原油上涨2.09美元或2.00%至106.80美元/桶。周末沙特遭遇胡塞武装袭击，包括周六对利雅得国际机场的袭击造成13人死亡、300多人受伤。高盛将12月布伦特油价预测从85美元上调至90美元，估计地缘风险目前为油价增加了逾20美元/桶的溢价。

US CPI inflation rises more than expected in September as gasoline surges
美国9月CPI通胀超预期上涨，汽油价格飙升推动
US consumer prices accelerated in September as the cost of gasoline surged and price pressures broadened beyond energy, data from the Labor Department's Bureau of Labor Statistics showed. Interest-rate traders are pricing in a 37% chance that Fed policymakers will raise rates this month, up from 22% on Tuesday, according to CME's FedWatch tool. Before the inflation data was released, Fed Vice Chair for Supervision Michelle Bowman said the central bank had time to wait and see how markets and the economy evolve after its September rate rise before deciding further action.
美国劳工部数据显示，9月消费者价格因汽油成本飙升而加速上涨，价格压力已扩散至能源以外领域。据CME FedWatch工具，利率交易员押注美联储本月加息的概率为37%，高于周二的22%。通胀数据发布前，美联储监管副主席Michelle Bowman表示，央行有时间观察9月加息后市场和经济的发展，再决定后续行动。
