SLAC 神经网络实现科学数据 100 倍无损压缩
SLAC Neural Network Achieves 100x Lossless Compression of Scientific Data
SLAC 国家加速器实验室研究人员在 Nature Machine Intelligence 发表方法，利用小波分析将数据按特征尺度分离，再由神经网络分别压缩不同尺度特征。该方法在保留传统压缩会丢失的精细信号（如 X 射线散斑图案）的同时实现 10-100 倍压缩。更关键的是支持选择性解压——仅检索感兴趣区域而非整个文件。
Researchers at SLAC National Accelerator Laboratory published a method in Nature Machine Intelligence that uses wavelet analysis to separate data by feature scale, then compresses different-scaled features via neural network separately. The method achieves 10-100x compression while preserving fine signals (like X-ray speckle patterns) that traditional compression erases. Crucially, it supports selective decompression—retrieving only regions of interest rather than entire files.
Why It Matters
下一代科学设施（如升级后的 LCLS）将产生每秒近 1TB 数据，远超当前存储和分析能力。这项工作表明 AI 不仅是分析工具，更是数据保管的底层基础设施。选择性解压对于需要反复回访历史数据集的研究者而言，可将检索时间从天级压缩到分钟级。
Next-generation scientific facilities (like the upgraded LCLS) will generate nearly 1TB per second, far exceeding current storage and analysis capacity. This work shows AI is not just an analysis tool but a foundational infrastructure for data preservation. Selective decompression could cut retrieval time from days to minutes for researchers who repeatedly revisit archived datasets.
