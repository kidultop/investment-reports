OpenAI said on Aug 18 that upcoming model Astra may meet its Critical cybersecurity capability threshold. The largest planned frontier RL run remains on hold pending stronger alignment evidence.

为什么重要

Why it matters

This is the first time a frontier lab has publicly paused a major training run because a model may reach a critical cyber capability threshold, showing evaluations and alignment assurances are becoming hard gates before deployment.

MIT TR | Princeton "shadow evaluation" finds AI agents still cannot do top-tier research autonomously

《麻省理工科技评论》报道了一项由普林斯顿大学主导的“影子评估”研究：研究者让 Claude Opus 4.8 在六天、3000 美元 API 额度和 GPU 预算内，尝试复现两篇未公开的 NeurIPS 2026 投稿论文的研究问题。结果两篇论文均被原始作者拒稿——智能体能完成工程实现、跑数百次实验，但在提出原创假设、判断实验设计、及时放弃失败路径等开放式研究判断上表现不佳。

MIT Technology Review covers a Princeton-led "shadow evaluation" in which Claude Opus 4.8 was given six days, $3,000 in API credits and GPU budget to reproduce research questions from two unpublished NeurIPS 2026 submissions. Both agent-written papers were rejected by the original authors: the agents could engineer experiments but lacked the creativity and judgment for open-ended research.

为什么重要

该研究为“递归自我改进”的狂热时间表按下刹车：工程能力不等于研究创造力，自主 AI 研究可能在短期内仍受限于判断与品味。

新加坡《刑事法（杂项修订）法案》生效：AI 生成未经同意私密影像入罪

Singapore's Criminal Law (Miscellaneous Amendments) Act takes effect, criminalising non-consensual AI-generated intimate images

新加坡《刑事法（杂项修订）法案》第二阶段于 8 月 17 日生效。新法将“未经同意制作私密影像，包括 AI 生成材料”定为刑事犯罪，最高可判两年监禁、罚款或两者兼施；涉及 14 岁以下未成年人者最高两年强制监禁，并可加罚或鞭刑。同时，法律扩大域外管辖，可起诉在海外诱骗并性侵未成年人的犯罪者。

The second tranche of Singapore's Criminal Law (Miscellaneous Amendments) Act 2025 took effect on 17 Aug 2026. It creates a new offence of producing intimate images, including AI-generated material, without consent, punishable by up to two years' imprisonment, a fine or both. Offences involving minors below 14 carry mandatory imprisonment and caning, and the law also expands extraterritorial jurisdiction over child sexual grooming and abuse.

为什么重要

新加坡将完全由 AI 合成的非同意私密影像明确入刑，并引入域外管辖，为全球生成式 AI 时代的未成年人保护与网络治理提供了立法参考。

Why it matters

By explicitly criminalising fully synthetic non-consensual intimate images and introducing extraterritorial jurisdiction, Singapore offers a legislative reference for child protection and online governance in the generative-AI era.
