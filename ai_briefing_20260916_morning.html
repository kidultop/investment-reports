Omni-Streaming Thinking：让流式多模态模型学会"暂缓下结论"

Omni-Streaming Thinking: curbing premature cross-modal commitment in streaming multimodal models
2026-09-15 · arXiv 2609.15128 · HKU / HKUST(GZ) / Sussex / Lightspeed
香港大学、港科大（广州）等团队提出 Omni-Streaming Thinking（OST），针对实时音视频模型的"过早跨模态承诺"问题：画面先出现机场登机口，模型便推断答案，几秒后广播音频给出相反信息，而早期误判已写入上下文。OST 将"当前证据"与"待验证假设"显式分离，配合结构化证据-预测-验证机制与答案门控，让模型把早期判断标记为待定而非事实。在冻结 Qwen3-Omni 骨干、仅加轻量适配的设置下，五个基准相对最强开放基线平均提升超 10%，并发布诊断基准 OST-DiagBench。
A HKU / HKUST(GZ) / Sussex / Lightspeed team proposes Omni-Streaming Thinking (OST), targeting "premature cross-modal commitment" in real-time audio-video models: a frame shows an airport gate and the model guesses, seconds later the audio contradicts it, but the early guess is already baked into context. OST explicitly separates "current evidence" from "hypotheses pending verification," with structured evidence-prediction-verification and answer gating. With the Qwen3-Omni backbone frozen and a lightweight adapter, OST improves over the strongest open baseline by over 10% on average across five benchmarks, and introduces the OST-DiagBench diagnostic suite.
为何重要 Why it matters：流式推理是实时智能体（客服、驾驶、翻译眼镜）的核心瓶颈，OST 证明"推迟承诺"本身可以作为可训练能力，且无需重训骨干——对低成本改造现有流式模型有直接工程价值。 / Streaming inference is the core bottleneck for real-time agents (support, driving, translation glasses); OST shows "deferred commitment" is itself a trainable capability upgradeable without retraining the backbone — immediately useful for retrofitting existing streaming models at low cost.
