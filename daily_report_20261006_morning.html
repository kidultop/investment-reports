Oil slips about 2% on rising Mideast crude exports and G7 emergency stocks release
中东原油出口回升叠加 G7 释储，国际油价跌近 2%，布伦特失守前险守 100 美元
Brent crude futures settled $1.93, or 1.89%, lower at $100.32 a barrel, while US WTI lost $1.68, or 1.84%, to $89.43, after Middle Eastern crude exports rose above pre-war levels in four of the seven days of the final week of September (Kpler data) and G7 nations agreed to release 100 million barrels of crude and diesel from emergency reserves. 布伦特原油结算价跌 1.93 美元（-1.89%）报 100.32 美元/桶，WTI 跌 1.68 美元（-1.84%）报 89.43 美元：Kpler 数据显示中东原油出口在 9 月最后一周 7 天中有 4 天超过战前水平，G7 亦同意释放 1 亿桶原油与柴油储备。但美伊战争引发的供应中断担忧限制跌幅：美国战略储备降至 2.83 亿桶（1982 年 10 月以来最低）、沙特阿美将 11 月亚洲官方售价下调至六年低位、OPEC+ 推迟 2027 年配额审查，VLCC 油轮运费飙升至约每日 130 万美元（约为 1 月的 43 倍）。

Schneider Electric confirms $22.6 billion all-cash deal to buy US software firm PTC
施耐德电气确认 226 亿美元全现金收购美国工业软件公司 PTC，创其史上最大并购
France's Schneider Electric agreed to buy PTC for $205 per share in an all-cash deal valuing the US software company's equity at about $22.6 billion, a 42.3% premium and its largest-ever acquisition, funded by 5-6 billion euros of new shares and 16-17 billion euros of new debt. PTC shares surged 33.5% while Schneider fell nearly 10%, wiping about 15 billion euros off its market value as investors weighed the premium and software valuations under AI pressure. The data-centre equipment giant expects 250 million euros of annual cost savings and 800 million euros of revenue synergies by year three; the deal closes by Q3 2027.
法国施耐德电气宣布以每股 205 美元全现金收购 PTC，股权价值约 226 亿美元、溢价 42.3%，为公司史上最大并购；交易将通过 50-60 亿欧元新股与 160-170 亿欧元新债融资。消息公布后 PTC 大涨 33.5%，施耐德则重挫近 10%、市值蒸发约 150 亿欧元，投资者质疑溢价与 AI 压力下的软件估值。这家数据中心设备巨头预计交易第三年起带来 2.5 亿欧元成本节约与 8 亿欧元收入协同，预计 2027 年三季度前完成交割。
