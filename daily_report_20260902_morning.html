Asian stocks set for declines as oil extends gains
油价续涨，亚洲股市料将下跌
Asian stocks were set to decline Wednesday as surging oil prices pushed bond yields higher and stoked fears that resurgent inflation will force policymakers to tighten. Dell Technologies bucked the tech weakness, jumping in extended trading after raising its annual sales forecast by $25 billion. Friday's U.S. payrolls report may offer the next clue on the policy outlook.

Global bonds extend selloff, oil prices surge on renewed US-Iran strikes
美伊再度互击，全球债市抛售延续、油价飙升
Global bond yields rose on Tuesday, extending a broad-market selloff in government debt fed by inflation fears, while oil prices surged to five-week highs after a new round of U.S. strikes against Iran. The Dow fell 419.02 points, or 0.8%, the S&P 500 lost 54.67 points, or 0.7%, and the Nasdaq Composite dropped 271.11 points, or 1%. Gold fell to a two-week low as Treasury yields and the dollar rose, with the dollar index up 0.27% at 99.68 and the yen at 160.19.

Global bond selloff sends yields to the highest level since 2008
全球债市抛售将收益率推至2008年以来最高
Global bond yields climbed back to the highest level in almost two decades on Tuesday as rising oil prices stoked inflation concerns and investors ramped up expectations for interest-rate hikes. The yield on a Bloomberg gauge of global sovereign bonds rose for a fourth straight session to 3.72%, the highest since mid-2008. The rate on 10-year Japanese government notes touched 3% for the first time since 1996, UK 30-year yields reached the highest since 1998 and the 10-year Treasury rate hit levels last seen in January last year, while two-year yields rose to 4.37%, the highest since February 2025. Traders are pricing an almost 70% chance the Federal Reserve hikes by a quarter-point this month, an increase from the European Central Bank is fully priced for next week, and they are all but certain the Bank of Japan will hike later this month. Federal Reserve Governor Michael Barr said the central bank should be prepared to raise interest rates if inflation fails to subside, warning price pressures are at risk of becoming entrenched after more than five years above target. Economists at Barclays and Societe Generale changed their Fed forecasts after Chair Kevin Warsh's hawkish speech on Friday, predicting hikes this year they previously had not anticipated.
周二全球债券收益率回升至近二十年最高水平，油价上涨引发通胀担忧，投资者加息预期升温。彭博全球主权债收益率指标连续第四个交易日上行至3.72%，为2008年中以来最高。日本10年期国债收益率自1996年以来首次触及3%，英国30年期收益率创1998年以来最高，美国10年期收益率升至去年1月以来水平，2年期收益率升至4.37%，为2025年2月以来最高。交易员定价本月美联储加息25个基点概率接近70%，欧洲央行下周加息已被完全定价，且几乎笃定日本央行本月晚些时候加息。美联储理事巴尔表示若通胀未能回落，应准备加息，并警告价格压力在连续五年超标后有固化风险。巴克莱与法国兴业银行在主席沃什上周五鹰派讲话后上调美联储预测，预计今年加息。

美国8月工厂活动放缓：ISM PMI 54.6低于预期55.2，JOLTS职位空缺727.1万
U.S. manufacturing activity slowed in August, with the Institute for Supply Management manufacturing index at 54.6 against expectations of 55.2, while the prices paid sub-index stood at 71.1, pointing to persistent cost pressures. The combination of cooling activity and sticky input costs complicates the Federal Reserve's policy path as it weighs a possible rate hike at its September meeting, with August payrolls on Friday expected to show employers added about 56,000 jobs.

ECB's Nagel Says Slowdown in Underlying Inflation Is Good News
欧洲央行纳格尔称核心通胀放缓是好消息
Slower underlying inflation in the euro zone in August is a positive development, according to European Central Bank Governing Council member Joachim Nagel. 'The good news is that the core inflation rate has declined along with services,' the Bundesbank president said Tuesday. 'We're not yet seeing second-round effects in the core inflation rate.' The comments come after Eurostat data showed euro area annual inflation rose back above 3% in August, driven largely by a 14.3% jump in energy costs, while core inflation held at 2.4%. Money markets are fully pricing in a further rate hike from the European Central Bank at this month's meeting.
欧洲央行管委、德国央行行长纳格尔表示，欧元区8月核心通胀放缓是积极进展。'好消息是核心通胀率与服务业通胀一同回落，'他周二表示，'我们尚未看到核心通胀出现第二轮效应。'此前欧盟统计局数据显示，欧元区8月同比通胀率因能源成本大涨14.3%而重回3%上方，但核心通胀维持在2.4%。货币市场已完全定价欧洲央行本月会议进一步加息。
