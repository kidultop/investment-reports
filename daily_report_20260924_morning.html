US stocks fall as 10-year Treasury yield hits highest since 2007
美股下跌，10年期美债收益率创2007年以来新高
US shares fell and benchmark 10-year Treasury yields climbed to 5.106%, their highest level since 2007, after data showed US business activity racing to a more-than-five-year high in September. The S&P 500 dropped 0.75%, the Nasdaq fell 1.13%, and the Dow Jones slipped 0.68%. Higher rates weighed on equities by raising financing costs and luring buyers back into bonds. Weak demand at a $70 billion 5-year note auction also sent yields higher.
美国股指下跌，基准10年期美债收益率攀升至5.106%，创2007年以来最高，此前数据显示9月美国商业活动创下逾五年新高。标普500跌0.75%，纳指跌1.13%，道指跌0.68%。高利率通过提高融资成本并吸引买家回流债市而打压股市。700亿美元5年期国债标售需求疲软也推高了收益率。

US Treasury to buy up to $6 billion in long-term bonds to curb rising yields
美国财政部将回购至多60亿美元长期国债以遏制收益率上升
The US Treasury announced plans to buy up to $6 billion in 20- to 30-year Treasuries on Thursday to curb the recent rise in borrowing costs. This is the second expanded buyback operation, after the first $6 billion cap on September 10 ultimately purchased about $5.2 billion. Treasury Secretary Bessent defended the plan, stating the Treasury acted after market prices 'deviated from equilibrium.' The 30-year yield hit 5.41%, a 2007 high.
美国财政部宣布将于周四回购至多60亿美元20至30年期国债，以遏制近期借贷成本上升。这是第二次扩大规模回购，9月10日首次60亿美元上限最终仅回购约52亿美元。财长贝森特为计划辩护，称财政部在市场价格'偏离均衡'后采取行动。30年期收益率触及5.41%，创2007年以来新高。
