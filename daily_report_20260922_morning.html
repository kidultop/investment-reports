Fed's Goolsbee warns fighting stubborn inflation will be 'painful', in contrast to Chair Warsh
美联储古尔斯比警告：压制顽固通胀过程"将很痛苦"，与主席沃什立场相左
By Christopher Rugaber, The Associated Press - Chicago Fed President Austan Goolsbee said Monday in London that the Fed faces persistent supply shocks from Iran-war oil prices and tariffs, and may have to push rates higher even at the cost of employment: "The only way to bring inflation down is to raise rates and narrow the gap between supply and demand." His remarks contrast with Fed Chair Kevin Warsh, who said after last week's first rate hike in three years to 3.75%-4.00% that he does not believe labor markets need to be harmed. Minneapolis Fed's Kashkari added inflation is broad-based, not just oil.
据美联社报道，芝加哥联储主席古尔斯比周一在伦敦表示，美联储面临伊朗战争油价与关税带来的持续性供给冲击，可能不得不以就业为代价推高利率："让通胀回落的唯一办法就是加息、缩小供需缺口。"此番表态与美联储主席沃什形成鲜明对比——沃什在上周三年来的首次加息（至3.75%-4.00%）后表示，无需以损害劳动力市场为代价。明尼阿波利斯联储卡什卡利补充称，通胀是全面性的，而非仅因油价。
