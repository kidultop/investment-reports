S&P closes at record high as soft jobs report eases rate-hike concerns
非农意外疲软缓解加息忧虑 标普500创收盘新高
U.S. stocks advanced on Friday, with the S&P closing at a record high to cap off a strong week of gains for the major indexes, after data showed the U.S. economy unexpectedly shed jobs last month and dampened expectations the Federal Reserve would raise interest rates at its September meeting. The Labor Department said nonfarm payrolls decreased by 23,000 jobs last month, well below the estimate of economists polled by Reuters that called for an increase of 80,000 jobs. Market expectations for a rate hike from the Fed at its next meeting dropped to about 44%, according to CME FedWatch, down from 55% in the prior session and 67% a week ago.
美国股市周五上涨，标普500指数创收盘新高，结束各大指数强劲的一周。此前数据显示美国上月意外减少就业岗位，缓解了市场对美联储9月加息的预期。美国劳工部公布，非农就业岗位减少2.3万个，远低于路透社调查经济学家预期的增加8万个。芝商所FedWatch显示，市场对美联储9月加息的预期从此前55%降至44%，一周前为67%。
