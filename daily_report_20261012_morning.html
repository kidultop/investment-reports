Trump says Russia to supply diesel to US and global markets; Treasury issues sanctions licence
特朗普宣布俄罗斯将向美国及全球市场供应柴油；美财政部发放制裁许可
President Donald Trump said Russia agreed to immediately supply more than 300,000 tons of diesel to the US and global marketplace, with further deliveries planned, to ease energy prices inflated by the Iran conflict. The US Treasury's OFAC issued a licence authorizing transactions involving Russian diesel, and the AP reported a six-month easing of diesel sanctions. Oil prices ended marginally higher with Brent settling at $104.72 (+0.42%) and WTI at $91.85 (+0.39%). Trump also said the US would not attack Iran before the November 3 midterm elections, though he later added: 'We may. I mean, we'll see.'
特朗普宣布与普京达成协议：俄罗斯将立即向美国及全球市场供应逾30万吨柴油，11月再供50万吨，以缓解美伊冲突推高的能源价格。美财政部OFAC发放涉俄柴油交易许可（美联社称有效期6个月）。油价尾盘小幅收高：布伦特结算 $104.72 (+0.42%)、WTI $91.85 (+0.39%)。特朗普重申11月3日中期选举前不会攻击伊朗，但次日又改口'可能会，走着瞧'，政策信号反复。

Focus now turns to Wednesday's September CPI for direction on Fed policy ahead of the October 27-28 meeting.
周五现货金涨超1.3%至约 $4,189/盎司，COMEX 12月黄金期货结算价 $4,216.30 (+1.43%)，白银 +2.73% 报 $61.05，受美元走软与上周深跌后的逢低买盘支撑。密歇根大学调查显示一年期通胀预期升至 4.7%，叠加中东局势，避险需求获得支撑。焦点转向周三 9 月 CPI，为 10 月 27-28 日 FOMC 会议定调。
