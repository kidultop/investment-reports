TaxiGPT 机制分析：行为失败不代表缺乏世界模型，Transformer 内部确实表征了地图与位置2026-09-22
World Modeling in Transformers: Behavioral Failures Can Hide a Faithful Internal Map (TaxiGPT)
arXiv cs.AI 新收录的机制可解释性研究以 TaxiGPT（在曼哈顿随机游走数据上训练的 Transformer）为对象：此前其导航失败被解读为「内部地图不一致」，但机制分析与因果干预显示，模型实际表征了路口与街道、追踪自身位置并使用「目标指南针」导航；失败源于叠加路口特征之间的相互干扰，破坏了内部地图中的定位。「可供性打包」（把具有相同合法移动的路口表征分组）可限制此类错误。作者还提出用于跨模型比较的机制性指标，显示世界建模能力在训练的不同阶段涌现，主张把问题从「是否有世界模型」转向机制地研究「世界建模」本身。
A new mechanistic-interpretability study on arXiv cs.AI revisits TaxiGPT, a transformer trained on random Manhattan walks whose navigation failures were read as an incoherent internal map. Mechanistic analysis and causal interventions show the model represents intersections and streets, tracks its position, and uses a goal compass; failures trace to interference between superposed intersection features, and "affordance packing" limits the damage. Mechanistic indicators show world-modeling capacities emerging at different training stages — motivating a shift from "does it have a world model" to mechanistically studying world modeling.
为何重要 Why it matters：为「LLM 是否具备世界模型」的争论提供了可操作的机制性证据与指标，也提示行为层评估可能系统性误判模型的内部能力——这对 Agent 安全评估方法同样适用。/ It turns a philosophical debate into measurable mechanistic evidence — and shows behavioral evals can misjudge internal capability.

GUARD：面向大推理模型的「自然遗忘」框架入选 EMNLP 2026，安全遗忘需规划 CoT 轨迹2026-09-22
GUARD: Natural Forgetting for Large Reasoning Models via Guided Answer-Reasoning Distillation (EMNLP 2026)
大推理模型（LRM）的机器遗忘比普通 LLM 更难：受保护事实或不安全推理可能先出现在思维链（CoT）中间轨迹中，再进入最终答案。GUARD（Guided Answer-Reasoning Distillation）提出遗忘不应只是「抑制内容」，而应学习一条自然的遗忘轨迹——连贯的不披露 CoT 加稳定的拒绝式答案：将模型生成的不安全披露转化为 safe-exit 轨迹，经 guidance tokens 对齐冻结模型后蒸馏，并提出 NFRS 指标衡量遗忘输出的结构稳定性、流畅度与「无依据替代内容」。在 R-TOFU 与有害意图设定上，GUARD 显著减少不安全与隐私披露，同时保留推理效用；代码已开源。
Machine unlearning is harder for large reasoning models: protected facts or unsafe rationales can surface in intermediate chain-of-thought traces before the final answer. GUARD argues forgetting should learn a natural trajectory — a coherent non-disclosing CoT followed by a stable refusal — by converting model-generated unsafe disclosures into safe-exit trajectories, aligning a frozen LRM via guidance tokens, and distilling the behavior. A new metric, NFRS, scores structural stability, fluency and unsupported substitutes. On R-TOFU and a harmful-intent setting, GUARD cuts unsafe and privacy disclosures while preserving reasoning utility; code is released.
为何重要 Why it matters：推理模型进入医疗、金融等强合规场景时，「答案安全但过程泄露」是真实风险；GUARD 给出了首批面向 CoT 全轨迹的遗忘方案与评测指标。/ Compliance-grade deployment of reasoning models needs process-level forgetting, not just answer suppression.
Unlearning benchmarks are finally grading the reasoning process, not just the output.

Hugging Face 提出「像物理学家一样剪枝」：把结构化剪枝重构为 Ising 优化问题2026-09-21
Pruning LLMs Like a Physicist: HF Recasts Structured Block Removal as an Ising Optimization Problem
Hugging Face 社区博客 9 月 21 日发布《Pruning LLMs Like a Physicist: Block Removal as an Ising Optimization Problem》，把删除连续参数块的结构化剪枝表述为 Ising 自旋优化：每个候选块的「保留/删除」对应自旋变量，目标函数显式编码块间交互，从而可用模拟退火等成熟求解器对压缩后架构做全局搜索，替代逐层贪心的重要性打分。文章强调结构化（块级）删除才能转化为实际推理节省，区别于需要专用内核支持的非结构化稀疏掩码。
A Sept 21 Hugging Face community post, "Pruning LLMs Like a Physicist: Block Removal as an Ising Optimization Problem," frames structured block removal as an Ising optimization: keep/remove decisions become spin variables with an objective that explicitly encodes inter-block interactions, letting solvers like simulated annealing search compressed architectures globally instead of greedy layer-importance scoring. Structured (block-level) removal is what actually translates into serving-time savings, unlike unstructured sparsity needing custom kernels.
为何重要 Why it matters：剪枝从「训练后处理」变成「全局优化问题」；若全局搜索显著优于贪心基线，开源模型的自部署成本曲线会再下移，对企业本地推理预算直接利好。/ Pruning as a global optimization problem could push open-model serving costs down another notch.
Compression is becoming an optimization job, not a training job.

Hugging Face announced that Jun Kim, creator and maintainer of oMLX, joins the company to support MLX — Apple's framework for local AI on Apple Silicon. oMLX stays Apache 2.0 under Jun's leadership, with a focus on streamlining the transformers-to-MLX model-definition path so new models reach Mac local inference faster, alongside continued collaboration with mlx-lm, mlx-vlm and LM Studio. With direct GGUF execution landing the same week, local-inference stacks are clearly converging with transformers.
为何重要 Why it matters：端侧生态从社区自组织转向机构化投入：HF 同时押注 llama.cpp（GGUF）与 MLX 两条本地路线，「新模型发布→本地可跑」的时间差会持续缩短。/ Institutional investment is flowing into local inference — the gap between a release and local runnability keeps shrinking.
Local AI is getting funded maintenance, not just hobbyist energy.
